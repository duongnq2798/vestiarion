Connect your workspace to a Discord channel, and the bills the agent holds or flags for a person are posted there after each cycle, each with a link to review it in Vestiarion. Nothing is decided in Discord: a link opens the bill in Approvals, and opening it approves nothing. Discord needs no app to install: an owner or admin pastes a channel webhook's URL once, and it works the same for a workspace on Arc testnet or on Arc mainnet.

## 1. Create a webhook in Discord

Someone who can manage the channel in your Discord server makes the webhook:

1. Open the channel's settings, then **Integrations**, then **Webhooks**.
2. Choose **New Webhook**. Give it a name your team will recognise, such as "Vestiarion", and check it posts to the channel you want. A private channel your finance people are in is a good choice: everyone in it sees the bills.
3. Choose **Copy Webhook URL**.

The URL is a password for that channel: anyone who has it can post there. Paste it only into Vestiarion, and do not share it in a chat.

## 2. Connect it in Vestiarion

An owner or admin connects Discord, once per workspace. Open **Settings** and find the **Discord** section, under Integrations.

1. Paste the URL into **Webhook URL**.
2. Choose **Connect**. Vestiarion asks Discord which channel the webhook posts to, posts a test message there that says it is checking the webhook, and saves the connection only once Discord took it. Settings then says "Discord is connected. A test message was posted to the channel."
3. The section now names the webhook and the channel's id, and says when it was connected. The ledger records `discord_connected`, with the webhook's and the channel's ids, never its URL.

Vestiarion accepts only a Discord webhook's URL. Anything else, a link to another site among them, is refused with "That is not a Discord webhook URL." and nothing is called. If the webhook was deleted in Discord, you see "Discord says this webhook does not exist." and nothing is saved. A workspace posts to one channel: to move to another, disconnect first, or Vestiarion says "Discord is already connected. Disconnect it first to post to another channel." If two people connect different channels at the same moment, only the first is saved, and the second sees "Someone connected another Discord channel to this workspace at the same moment, so this one was not saved. The test message in this channel can be ignored."

**Connect** and **Send test message** each call Discord, so they allow a few tries a minute for a workspace and for a person. Past that, Vestiarion says "Too many tries at once. Try again in a minute." and calls nothing.

Anyone else in the workspace sees whether Discord is connected, and "An owner or admin can connect Discord." On a deployment that has not run the migration that adds Discord yet, the section says "Discord is not set up on this deployment yet."

## 3. What the channel receives

After each cycle, when the agent has held or flagged a bill for a person, or asked for details such as a purchase order, since the last message, the channel gets one message listing those bills:

```text
**Acme Studio** · 2 bills wait for approval

• **1,250.00 USDC** · Northwind Hosting · held: Code stopped it: it is above the counterparty's payment limit. · needs two approvals, an accountant can co-sign · Review: <https://www.vestiarion.xyz/o/acme/approvals#payable-…>
• **75.50 EURC** · Jiren Studio · flagged: The bank details on the invoice differ from the ones on file. · Review: <https://www.vestiarion.xyz/o/acme/approvals#payable-…>
Opening a link never approves anything.
```

- Each bill takes one line: the amount and its currency, the payee, whether the agent held or flagged it or it is waiting for details, why, and **Review:** with the bill's own link. A bill waiting for details opens on Approvals too, where an owner or admin adds them with **Add details**.
- When a check in code stopped a bill, such as its payment limit, the payee lock or the cash it needs, the line says what stopped it, as its row on **Bills & receivables** does: "Code stopped it: it is above the counterparty's payment limit." It never repeats the agent's own words that it meant to pay. Otherwise, the line gives the first sentence of the agent's reason.
- A bill above the workspace's figure for two approvals says "needs two approvals". While an owner lets accountants co-sign, it says "needs two approvals, an accountant can co-sign".
- Only bills that still wait are posted: one a person approved, rejected or returned between the agent's decision and the message is left out. A payment held for your verdict in shadow mode is not posted: you settle it with **Give your verdict**.
- A message lists at most ten bills, as many as fit in Discord's 2,000 characters, then "…and 3 more in Approvals", with a link to Approvals, for the rest.
- The channel is told only about bills held after Discord was connected.

Every value from a bill, such as a payee's name or the agent's reason, is shown as plain text: it cannot become a link, a heading or a mention, and the message pings no one, not even `@everyone`. A wallet address is shortened to its first and last four characters, and Discord shows no preview of a link.

## 4. Review a bill

**Review** opens the bill's card on **Approvals** in Vestiarion. Discord's links carry no token: you sign in as usual, and the card shows what you can do with your role. Opening a link never approves anything. You decide the bill there, with **Approve and pay**, **Reject** or **Return to agent**, under every check Vestiarion makes; Discord never decides a payment.

## 5. Send a test message, and see what went wrong

Once connected, an owner or admin can choose **Send test message** to post a test message to the channel. Settings answers "A test message was posted to the Discord channel."

When Discord does not take a message:

- Discord down, or not reached: the bills are posted again with the next cycle, and nothing is lost. The section shows **Last error**, "Discord could not be reached. The bills are posted again with the next cycle.", or the status Discord answered, with when.
- Discord asking Vestiarion to slow down: a wait of up to 2 seconds is waited out at once, and a longer one leaves the message to the next cycle.
- Discord refusing the message itself: **Last error** says why and "Those bills wait in Approvals.", and the next message carries on with new bills, so one message Discord refuses cannot stop the rest.

The next message Discord takes clears **Last error**.

## 6. Disconnect

An owner or admin chooses **Disconnect**, then **Disconnect Discord** to confirm. Settings answers "Discord is disconnected. Bills waiting for approval are no longer posted there." Vestiarion deletes the webhook's URL, and the ledger records `discord_disconnected`.

The webhook itself belongs to your Discord server: it stays in the channel's settings until someone deletes it there. Delete it in Discord too if you no longer want anything able to post through it.

If someone deletes the webhook in Discord first, Vestiarion disconnects it the next time it posts or sends a test message, and the cycle says "Discord said the workspace's channel webhook is gone, so Discord was disconnected". After **Send test message**, the section switches to **Connect** and says beside it "Discord says this webhook no longer exists, so Discord was disconnected. Connect a new webhook." The ledger records `discord_disconnected` as the system's, with no person.

## What Discord receives

The workspace's name, and for each bill posted, the payee's name, the amount, the agent's reason and a link to the bill in Vestiarion. A wallet address only shortened to its first and last four characters; never an email, a key or a full address. Vestiarion reads no message in Discord: when you connect, it asks Discord only for the webhook's name and its channel's and server's ids, and after that it only posts to the one channel the webhook belongs to.

## For the person who runs the deployment

Discord needs no setting and no app: it works once migration `0140_discord.sql` has run. Before that, the section says Discord is not set up, and the cycle's `discord` stage does nothing. The webhook's URL is kept encrypted under the master key, and Vestiarion only ever calls it at `https://discord.com/api/webhooks/`, rebuilt from the webhook's id and token, never at the address pasted.
