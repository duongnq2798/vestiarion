Add one of your bills, let Vestiarion's agent decide on it, and read why, before any money moves. You need an email address and one bill you have received: no wallet, no USDC and no API key. It takes about five minutes.

<Callout kind="tip" title="Start in shadow mode">
In shadow mode you keep paying the way you do today. The agent decides on the same bills, and nothing it decides is paid until a person agrees. In a new workspace on Arc testnet, with no wallet, a payment you agree to is simulated.
</Callout>

## 1. Sign in

Open [www.vestiarion.xyz/login](https://www.vestiarion.xyz/login). Choose **Continue with Google**, or enter a **Work email** and choose **Email me a sign-in link**, then open the link it sends. There is no password, and the same link creates your account the first time.

## 2. Create a workspace in shadow mode

A workspace holds one business's suppliers, bills, decisions and payments. Under **Workspace name**, type your business's name.

- Keep **Run alongside how you pay today (shadow mode)** ticked. It is ticked already when you come from **Try it on your bills** on the home page.
- If you are asked which kind of workspace, choose **Test workspace**, on Arc testnet. A **Production workspace** is on Arc mainnet and moves real USDC once an owner takes it live; it has no shadow mode.

Choose **Create workspace**. The workspace starts as a sandbox: nothing reaches a chain until you take it live. You land on **Treasury**, the console, where the **Get started in shadow mode** checklist lists **Add your suppliers**, **Add your real bills** and **Give your first verdict**, and the wallet after them, under **To pay on Arc**.

## 3. Add a supplier

Open **Counterparties**. The **Add counterparty** form is open on an empty workspace.

- **Legal or trading name**: the supplier as it appears on its bills.
- **Role**: **Vendor** for a supplier, **Contractor** for a person you pay for work.
- **Payment limit (USDC)**: the most the agent may pay this supplier on one bill without a person. A bill above it is held for you.
- **Payment address**: leave it empty. It reads **Optional until payment setup**: a sandbox simulates the payment.
- **Does this supplier send you purchase orders?** **Yes** means a bill waits until its purchase order is on file; **No** means it does not, though the goods or services still need to be marked received.

Choose **Add and screen**. The supplier is screened as soon as it is saved, and a message gives the result, such as "Northwind Hosting added and screened: clear risk."

For example, a hosting supplier called Northwind Hosting, a **Vendor** with a limit of 500.00 USDC, that sends no purchase orders. The name and the figures are an example; use your own.

## 4. Add one of your bills

Open **Bills & receivables**. On an empty workspace the **New invoice** form is open.

- **Counterparty**: the supplier you just added.
- **Amount** and **Currency**: as on the bill. A new shadow workspace takes bills in USDC; for bills in your own currency, see [Run alongside how you pay today](/docs/guides/shadow-mode#1-turn-it-on).
- **Due date**: the bill's due date.
- **PO reference**: the purchase order's number, if the supplier sends them.
- Tick **Goods or services received** once they have arrived.

Choose **Add invoice**. As the form says: "The agent usually decides on a payable within a minute of adding it." You can also drop in a PDF, import a CSV or forward the bill by email: see [Your first payment](/docs/guides/first-payment#read-an-invoice-from-a-document) and [Add invoices by email](/docs/guides/email-invoices).

## 5. See what the agent decided

The bill's card in **Bills & receivables** shows the decision once the agent has run, usually within a minute; an owner or admin can also choose **Run cycle now** on the console. The agent can:

- decide to pay it now, which in shadow mode waits for your verdict: its card reads **Waiting for your verdict**;
- schedule it for a later day, such as an early-payment discount's last day or the due date;
- hold it for a person, when a check in code stops it, such as a bill above the supplier's limit;
- flag it, when it repeats a bill already paid;
- or ask for what is missing, such as a purchase order or the goods received.

A decision to pay is not a payment. In shadow mode nothing is paid until a person agrees, and in any workspace every payment passes the checks in [How payments are controlled](/docs/guides/controls) first.

## 6. Read why

On the card, **Agent’s reasoning** says why, citing the facts the agent was given: the amount, the due date, the purchase order, the supplier's risk and limit. Below it are the evidence it checked and its ledger entry. When the model wanted to pay and a rule in code stopped it, the card says "Blocked by code, not by the model" and names the rule in words. **How the agent decided** opens the decision step by step, from the signed entries.

Every decision is an entry in the workspace's **Audit log**, signed and linked to the one before it. Choose **Verify hash chain** there: when every signature and link holds, it answers "Chain intact".

## 7. Agree or disagree

A decision in shadow mode asks **Do you agree with the agent?**

- **Agree and pay** confirms "You agree with the agent, and it is paid now, simulated in this sandbox". Your verdict and the simulated payment are recorded.
- **Disagree** asks why, in a few words, and the bill is not paid: the agent decides it again later, or you reject it.

Keep paying the bill the way you do today. Over time, the console's **Shadow mode** section shows how often you agreed with the agent.

## What to do next

### Keep running in shadow mode

Add each bill as it arrives and give your verdict on each decision. Nothing is paid from your accounts. When you want each payment you agree to made in USDC on Arc testnet, at the bill's amount, take the workspace live on Arc testnet: [Run alongside how you pay today](/docs/guides/shadow-mode) shows how, with test USDC for your real amounts.

### Prepare to go live

<Callout kind="real-money" title="Live payments move USDC">
Once a workspace is live, the agent pays bills that pass every check on its own, within the limits you set, and holds the rest for a person. On Arc mainnet that is real USDC. Read [How payments are controlled](/docs/guides/controls) before you take a workspace live.
</Callout>

[Go live](/docs/guides/go-live) creates the workspace's wallets and funds them. On Arc testnet a wallet is one click away and USDC comes from Circle's faucet; on Arc mainnet the treasury can be a wallet you hold. Then [Your first payment](/docs/guides/first-payment) follows a payment to the explorer.

## Try it with sample data instead

No bill at hand? In an empty sandbox the console offers **Try it with sample data**. Choose **Load sample data**. It adds six example counterparties with invoices and milestones, chosen so that one cycle shows every outcome. Within about a minute the agent runs that cycle on its own. The console then shows what it decided:

- a hosting invoice with a purchase order and goods received is paid;
- an annual support plan with a 2% early-payment discount is scheduled for its discount deadline;
- an overage with no purchase order waits for information;
- an invoice over the counterparty's payment limit is held for a person;
- a print run billed again under a purchase order that was already paid is flagged;
- a verified contractor milestone is released, and an unverified one waits.

In **Approvals**, the held and flagged ones wait for a person: choose **Approve and pay**, then **Pay now**, on the held one. In a sandbox the payment is simulated, and the ledger records that you approved it. When the list is empty it reads "Nothing is waiting for a decision."

On the console, **Next 30 days** lists each day money is due or expected, with the balance after it. Under it, **What if clients pay late?** shows what late or missing receivables would do over 30, 60 or 90 days: see [Plan for clients paying late](/docs/guides/cash-scenarios).

When you are done, choose **Remove sample data** on the console. A workspace with sample data loaded cannot connect Circle: "Remove the sample data first. It exists only to try the agent with simulated payments."
