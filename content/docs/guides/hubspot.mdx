Connect HubSpot, and your won deals come into Vestiarion with their customers, so you plan their collections without typing them again. A won deal becomes a [deal](/docs/guides/deals) with its collection schedule only when you plan it, from the New deal form filled in for you. A deal changed in HubSpot afterwards says so, and nothing in Vestiarion changes until you do.

Vestiarion reads from HubSpot and writes nothing to it.

## Before you start

- **An owner or admin** of the workspace connects HubSpot. Anyone else sees "An owner or admin connects HubSpot."
- **A HubSpot account** with deals: a developer test account works for a first try. One HubSpot account connects to one Vestiarion workspace at a time.
- **The deals' currency.** Vestiarion plans deals in USDC or EURC. A deal in US dollars is planned in USDC, and one in euros in EURC, one for one. A deal in another currency is planned in that currency only in a workspace in [shadow mode](/docs/guides/shadow-mode), on Arc testnet; otherwise you type its total in USDC.

## 1. Connect HubSpot

Open **Settings**. Under **HubSpot**, choose **Connect HubSpot**.

The connect page names the workspace (or lets you pick one, if you manage several) and says what Vestiarion does with HubSpot's data:

- It reads the companies, contacts and deals of the account you connect: names, amounts, close dates, pipeline stages and each company's main contact email. It writes nothing to HubSpot.
- They are shown to the workspace's members and to its agent. The agent's model provider may see a deal's facts to decide on its collections, and may not train on them.
- Vestiarion never uses HubSpot data to train or improve any AI model, and never passes it to anyone else without your workspace's say.

Tick **I agree**, then choose **Continue to HubSpot**. HubSpot asks you to pick the account and to allow access. Your agreement, with its date, is kept on the connection and in the workspace's signed ledger.

Back in Settings, the card says "HubSpot is connected. Its won deals are coming in on Bills & receivables, under Deals." The ledger records `accounting_connected`, with `provider: "hubspot"`.

If the account is already connected to another workspace, the card says "This HubSpot account is connected to another Vestiarion workspace." Disconnect it there first.

## 2. Which deals come in

The first sync starts as soon as you connect. It brings in your open deals, and the deals won in the last 90 days, with each deal's company and the email of that company's main contact. After that, Vestiarion asks HubSpot every 15 minutes for the deals that changed, and **Sync now** asks at once.

- **A deal won before the last 90 days** stays out, until someone changes it in HubSpot.
- **A lost deal** comes in only if Vestiarion already had it.
- **Each deal comes in once**, by its id in HubSpot, whatever changes in it.

## 3. Plan a won deal's collections

Open **Bills & receivables**. Under **Deals**, "From HubSpot: 4 won deals" lists each won deal with its company, amount, close day and stage:

- **Plan collections**, for a won deal with an amount and a company;
- why it cannot be planned, for one without: "No amount in HubSpot" or "No company in HubSpot";
- **Planned**, with **Open the deal**, for a deal you planned already. A deal is never planned twice.

Below, "Not won yet" lists the deals still open in HubSpot, for what is coming. They cannot be planned until they are won.

**Plan collections** opens the New deal form, filled in from the deal:

- **Customer**: the customer this HubSpot company was planned for before; else the customer with the company's name; else the company as a new customer, added when you confirm. When two customers have the name, you choose.
- **Deal**: the deal's name.
- **Total** and **Currency**: the deal's amount, in USDC for a deal in US dollars and EURC for one in euros. The form says "HubSpot says 12,500 USD: planned as 12,500.00 USDC, one for one."
- **Start date**: the day the deal closed.
- **External reference**: the deal's id in HubSpot, which links the two. It cannot be changed here.

Pick how it is billed (a deposit and balance, milestones, a monthly retainer or your own lines), check the schedule, and choose **Confirm deal**. The deal is signed in the ledger as any deal, as `deal_signed` with `via: "hubspot"` and the deal's ids in HubSpot. The company is linked to the customer, so the next deal from it finds the customer.

## 4. When a deal changes in HubSpot

A planned deal can change in HubSpot afterwards: its amount, its close date, its stage, or it is deleted. Vestiarion never changes the deal for it. The deal's page says **Changed in HubSpot**, with each change:

- "Amount: 12,000 USD to 15,000 USD."
- "Close date: Sep 20, 2026 to Sep 30, 2026."
- "Marked lost in HubSpot (Closed Lost). Nothing is cancelled: cancel the deal if it is off."
- "Deleted in HubSpot. Nothing is cancelled: cancel the deal if it is off."

The ledger records each new set of changes once, as `crm_deal_changed`. Then choose:

- **Change deal**, which opens the deal's Change deal form filled in: HubSpot's new amount as the new total, a correction proposed for the lines not invoiced yet, and the changes as the reason. Check it and save it as a new version. Lines already released, and their receivables, stay as they are. The version is signed as `deal_revised` with `via: "hubspot"`.
- **Keep the deal as it is**, when the deal is right as it stands. The changes are marked as seen, and the ledger records `crm_deal_change_kept`.

Both act on the changes the page showed you. If HubSpot changed the deal again since you opened the page, nothing is saved: reload the page to see what changed now.

A deal marked lost in HubSpot is only flagged. To stop its lines, use **Cancel deal** on its page.

## 5. Billing emails

A customer linked to a HubSpot company that has no billing email yet is listed under "Billing emails from HubSpot", with the email of the company's main contact. Choose **Use this email** if it is where invoices go: it becomes the customer's billing email, used for reminders once you turn them on for an invoice. The ledger records `counterparty_notice_email_changed` with `via: "hubspot"`. A billing email the customer already has is never replaced.

## Status and errors

The HubSpot card in Settings says how the connection is doing:

- "HubSpot needs you to reconnect", when the app was uninstalled in HubSpot or its access removed. Choose **Reconnect HubSpot**.
- "HubSpot asked Vestiarion to slow down. Syncing again at 14:05 UTC." when HubSpot's limits were reached. Nothing is lost: the next sync goes on where this one stopped.
- "HubSpot is not set up on this deployment yet." on a deployment where HubSpot is not configured.

## Disconnect

Choose **Disconnect HubSpot** on the card. Vestiarion stops reading at once, ends its access in HubSpot and deletes its tokens. Deals already planned stay in Vestiarion, with their receivables. The ledger records `accounting_disconnected`.
