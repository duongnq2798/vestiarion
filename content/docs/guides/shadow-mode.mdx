Shadow mode lets a business try the agent on its real bills without moving its money. You keep paying your bills as you do today, in your own currency. The agent reads the same bills and decides on each one, as it would in any workspace, and you agree or disagree with every decision. Each payment you agree to is made in USDC on Arc testnet, at the bill's amount in USDC. Over time the console shows how often you agreed with the agent.

## Before you start

- **A workspace on Arc testnet**, live, with USDC in its operating wallet: see [Go live](/docs/guides/go-live). Each payment you agree to is paid from that wallet, so keep it funded from Circle's faucet.
- **The owner of the workspace.** Only an owner turns shadow mode on or off. Anyone who can approve payments (an owner, admin or approver) gives verdicts.

Shadow mode is for Arc testnet only. On Arc mainnet the panel says "Shadow mode runs on Arc testnet. On Arc mainnet the agent pays your real bills." Once you trust the agent, a small share of your bills can move to a workspace on Arc mainnet, which pays them in real USDC.

## 1. Turn it on

Open **Settings**. Under **Workspace**, the **Shadow mode** section says it is off. Choose **Your currency**: USDC, chosen at first, where you enter each bill's amount in USDC, or the currency your bills are written in, such as EUR. Then choose **Turn on shadow mode**. From then on the section says since when it is on, and for which currency, and that the agent pays nothing until a person agrees.

The console's **Get started in shadow mode** checklist then walks you through the rest and ticks each step as you take it: a wallet, funded and live; your suppliers; your real bills; and your first verdict. It hides once you give that verdict.

## 2. Add your bills as you do today

Add each bill as a payable in AP / AR. In USDC, enter its **Amount** in USDC, as on any invoice. In another currency, the invoice form's **Currency** starts at it: type the **Amount** as it is written on the bill, such as 1,250.00 for a bill in EUR. That bill is paid in USDC at the day's rate, which comes from ExchangeRate-API (**Rates By Exchange Rate API**), and a US dollar is one USDC. Its card shows it as written, with the rate and the day of the rate, beside the USDC it is paid in. Outside shadow mode, the form takes only USDC and EURC.

A bill read from a document, an email or a chat, or sent through the API, is added in USDC or EURC: for a bill in another currency, type its amount into the form instead.

The agent decides on each bill in its next cycle, as in any workspace. It may pay it now, schedule it for a later day, hold it, flag it, or ask for what is missing. If a supplier sends you no purchase orders, turn purchase orders off on its row in Counterparties, so its bills are not held for one.

## 3. Give a supplier with no Arc address a mirror address

Most of the people you pay have no wallet on Arc. A payment you agree to still needs an address to go to, so in shadow mode a supplier with no address can be given a mirror address: a wallet Vestiarion makes for it on Arc testnet, in this workspace's Circle wallets.

In **Counterparties**, open the supplier's row and choose **Give it a mirror address**. The row then shows the address, and says: "Mirror address: a wallet Vestiarion made for this payee on Arc testnet, in shadow mode." No person typed it, so its first payment does not wait for a second person to confirm the address. The USDC you agree to pay a supplier stays in its mirror wallet, on Arc testnet.

If the supplier later gives you an Arc address of its own, enter it as you would any change: it replaces the mirror address, the row no longer calls it one, and the next payment waits for a person to confirm it.

## 4. Agree or disagree with each decision

When the agent decides to pay a bill now, nothing is sent: the payment is held for your verdict. In Approvals, AP / AR and the console, its card asks **Do you agree with the agent?**, and says what agreeing pays: the amount in USDC, the payee and the payee's address.

The same holds when a check in code stopped the payment the agent decided, such as a first payment to an address only you stand behind, or one above the payee's limit. The card says why, and still asks for your verdict: **Agree and pay** pays it through the same checks as **Approve and pay**, and nothing else settles it until a verdict is given. So every payment made in shadow mode has a verdict behind it.

- **Agree and pay** asks you to confirm: "You agree with the agent, and it is paid in USDC on Arc testnet now", with that payment. It then goes through the same checks as **Approve and pay**, to the address the card showed: if the payee's address changed since, nothing is paid. Your agreement is recorded once the payment goes, or its transfer was tried. If the payment is refused, nothing is recorded: agree and pay again once it can be paid.
- **Disagree** asks **Why do you disagree?**, in at most 280 characters. Then choose what happens to the bill: **Decide it again later** returns it to the agent for its next cycle, and **Do not pay it** rejects it.

If you may not pay it yourself, for example because you entered the bill and another person can approve payments, the card offers **Agree** alone and says why. Your agreement is recorded, and another person pays it in Approvals.

In Slack and Telegram, the agent's message about a payment held for your verdict, and the list of what waits for a person, say it waits for your verdict, and **Give your verdict** opens it here. Slack offers no buttons on it: until someone gives a verdict, nothing else settles it, and **Approve and pay** refuses a payment someone disagreed with, unless its transfer had already left: that is recorded.

If an **Agree and pay** stops before it finishes, the card says **Unfinished decision**. After ten minutes it offers **Agree and pay** and **Disagree** again, and nothing else settles the payment until a verdict is given.

Every other decision, such as a payment scheduled for a later day, or a bill the agent itself held, flagged or asked about, offers **Agree** and **Disagree** on its card in AP / AR. Its own actions stay where they are. When a scheduled payment's day comes, the agent decides again, and that payment is held for your verdict.

Each decision takes one verdict, and the card shows it once given. A second verdict on the same decision changes nothing.

## 5. See how often you agreed

The console shows a **Shadow mode** section while shadow mode is on. It says how many of the agent's decisions you agreed with, out of the verdicts given, as a share. It also says how many decisions still wait for your verdict, with **See them in AP / AR** to give them.

Vestiarion's public numbers at [/open](https://www.vestiarion.xyz/open) count how often customers agreed with the agent in shadow mode, across every workspace, as a share. They never show a workspace's name, a bill or a verdict's reason.

## 6. Turn it off

In **Settings**, choose **Turn off shadow mode** and confirm. The agent pays on its own again, within its limits. A payment waiting for a person to agree stays held until a person decides it. Decide it in Approvals with **Approve and pay**, **Reject** or **Return**, as any held payment: no verdict can be given on it once shadow mode is off.

## What the ledger records

Each step is a signed entry in the workspace's ledger, and reaches webhook endpoints as `ledger.appended`:

- `shadow_mode_started` and `shadow_mode_ended`, with who turned it on or off and the currency.
- `ap_pay` for a payment held in shadow mode, with `execution.heldBecause: "shadow_verdict"`. It is not a refusal by code: the decision passed every check.
- `counterparty_address_changed` with `via: "mirror"` when a supplier is given a mirror address: who asked, the address and its Circle wallet id.
- `decision_verdict` for each verdict: who gave it, the decision it is about (`entrySeq`), the payable (`subjectId`), the agent's action, `agree` or `disagree`, and the reason.

## If something goes wrong

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Choose USDC, or the currency your bills are written in as a three-letter code, such as EUR. | Choose your currency from the list. |
| Shadow mode is already on. | Someone turned it on a moment before. Reload the page. |
| Shadow mode is already off. | Someone turned it off a moment before. Reload the page. |
| A cycle is running. Try again in a minute, once it has finished. | The agent read the workspace's settings when its cycle began. Try again once it ends. |
| Verdicts are given in shadow mode. An owner turns it on in Settings. | Shadow mode is off. An owner turns it on in **Settings**. |
| That decision came before shadow mode started. | Verdicts are given on decisions made in shadow mode only. |
| Say why you disagree, in a few words. | Enter your reason, then choose **Disagree** again. |
| Keep the reason to 280 characters. | Shorten your reason. |
| It no longer waits for your verdict, so nothing was paid. | The bill was paid, returned or decided again since. Your agreement is recorded; the card shows where it stands. |
| A verdict was given on this decision already | Someone gave a verdict on it a moment before. The card shows which. |
| This payee has an address already. A mirror address is for a payee with none. | The supplier has an address: payments go there. |
| Go live on Arc testnet first: a mirror address is a wallet in this workspace's Circle wallets. | Take the workspace live on Arc testnet in **Settings**, under **Go live**, then give the mirror address. |
| A mirror address is for shadow mode. An owner turns it on in Settings. | Shadow mode was turned off after the page loaded. Reload the page. |
| This workspace's Circle credentials are stored but could not be read. | Vestiarion cannot use this workspace's Circle wallets just now. Try again later; nothing was given. |
| This decision waits for a verdict in shadow mode. Agree or disagree with it in Approvals first. | Give your verdict on its card in **Approvals**: **Agree and pay**, or **Disagree**. |
| Someone disagreed with this decision in shadow mode, so it is not paid. Return it to the agent or reject it. | A payment someone disagreed with is not made. Return it to the agent, or reject it, in **Approvals**. |
