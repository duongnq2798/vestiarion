Vestiarion is an AI agent for bills and payments, for small businesses. It reads each bill your team adds, decides whether to pay it now, pay it on a later day, hold it for a person or ask for what is missing, and writes down why. Rules in code check every payment before money moves, within limits you set, and every decision is signed into a ledger you can verify. Payments are in USDC on Arc: Arc testnet to try everything, Arc mainnet for real money.

These docs take a business from its first decision to live payments, and show developers how to build on the API and webhooks.

## What you can build

- **A bot that reports held payments.** List [invoices](/docs/api/list-invoices) and [milestones](/docs/api/list-milestones) with `status=held`, and post each one with its `agentReasoning`: the agent's own account of why it did not pay.
- **An accounting sync.** Page through [invoices](/docs/api/list-invoices) with `direction=payable` or `direction=receivable`, and copy each one's status, amount, counterparty, settlement time and `txHash` into your books.
- **An audit mirror.** Copy the [ledger](/docs/api/list-ledger-entries) once, keep it current with [webhooks](/docs/webhooks), and check each entry's [signature](/docs/webhooks/verify) yourself. [Verify the ledger](/docs/api/verify-ledger) to compare your result with Vestiarion's.
- **An invoice feed.** [Add invoices from your own system](/docs/guides/api-invoices), such as an accounting tool or a billing script, and let the agent decide each one. For bills kept in Xero or QuickBooks Online, bring them in [from Xero](/docs/guides/xero) or [from QuickBooks](/docs/guides/quickbooks) with no code. For deals won in HubSpot or Pipedrive, bring them in [from HubSpot](/docs/guides/hubspot) or [from Pipedrive](/docs/guides/pipedrive) and plan their collections. For contractor work delivered in Asana, [accept it from Asana](/docs/guides/asana) against the criteria you agreed, and the agent pays it once a person has.
- **A treasury dashboard.** Show the [treasury](/docs/api/get-treasury)'s account balances, reserve position, obligations due within 7 and 14 days and the latest liquidity forecast. Read [status](/docs/api/get-status) first, so the dashboard shows whether payments are live or simulated.

## What the API is

Every endpoint is under `https://www.vestiarion.xyz/api/v1`. Most read; four add records, with a key given write access: a counterparty, an invoice, a milestone or a payee link. No request moves money, approves a payment, or changes or removes a record: the agent decides what is added as it decides anything typed into the console. Each request carries a [workspace API key](/docs/get-started/authentication) and reaches only that key's workspace. The [endpoint overview](/docs/api) lists every endpoint, and [data delivery methods](/docs/data-delivery) compares pulling with the API to receiving webhooks.
