# Vestiarion v0.1.0-beta.15 — Earned vendors, approve many, Linear, Google Drive and n8n

The fifteenth beta of Vestiarion. In shadow mode, an owner can now set a rule for when a vendor has earned their
trust, and let the agent pay vendors that earned it on its own, on Arc testnet, under every check a live payment
passes. On Approvals, several held bills can be approved with one confirmation, and paid in one Arc transaction where
the operating wallet can send them that way. Contractor work delivered in Linear is accepted by a person before its
milestone is paid. Bills dropped into a Google Drive folder wait for review. The accountant console lists four more
kinds of work waiting across the workspaces an accountant serves. A fifth free tool checks a batch payout before it is
sent. For builders: MCP tools for evidence, receipts and
close packs, CLI 0.2.0 commands to export and verify them, and n8n nodes published with npm provenance.
Also in this release:
- production's public pages are checked about every 10 minutes from outside Vestiarion's hosting;
- the audit log and Insights send far less to open;
- the Quality Lab replays 55 of the team's own shadow-mode decisions, up from 46, with 0 wrong payments.

## Highlights

- **Earned vendors, in shadow mode (#541, #543, #544).** Shadow mode runs the agent on a workspace's real bills while
  the business keeps paying them as it does today: each payment the agent decides waits for a person to agree or
  disagree.
  - **The rule (#541).** In **Settings**, under **Earned vendors**, an owner sets how many Agree verdicts in a row a
    vendor needs, from 5 to 50. A disagreement, a bill flagged for review or a check in code stopping one of its bills
    starts the count again. The report and the live slice's vendor list show which vendors earned it, with the
    verdicts behind each. Each change and each vendor that earns or loses it is a signed ledger entry.
  - **Paid on its own (#543).** An owner may then choose **Let the agent pay earned vendors**. A payment to an earned
    vendor that passed every check is made at once, in test USDC on Arc testnet, and the verdict is given after.
    Every limit still applies, the agent's spending limit included. One disagreement takes the status away, and once
    3 of a vendor's bills paid this way wait for a verdict, its next payment waits too. Off by default, and refused
    on Arc mainnet.
  - **Turning it on works (#544).** Turning the switch on failed in production before this fix.
  - Proven in the team's own test workspace on Arc testnet: the agent paid three earned vendor bills on its own,
    2.25, 2.10 and 9.80 test USDC, from Oct 29 to Oct 31. That is our own testing, not a customer's.
- **Approve several bills at once (#546).** On **Approvals**, tick from 2 to 20 bills you could pay now in USDC.
  - One confirmation lists each payee, address and amount, and the total.
  - Each bill is still approved as **Approve and pay** approves it alone, with every check, its own signed entry and
    its own receipt.
  - The ones that pay now are sent together, in one Arc transaction, where the operating wallet can send them that way.
  - A bill that cannot be taken together, such as a bank payout or EURC, is refused by name and the others are still
    decided.
- **Contractor work accepted from Linear (#548, #549).** An owner or admin connects Linear, read-only, and links a
  milestone to the Linear issue its work is delivered in, with acceptance criteria written in Vestiarion.
  - When the issue is done, a person checks the deliverables against the criteria and accepts the work. Only then
    does the agent decide the payment, under every guardrail. A done issue alone never pays.
  - Reopening, canceling, archiving or deleting the issue, or changing its deliverables, takes the acceptance back
    when Vestiarion reads the change before the payment is sent.
  - A link pasted into the issue counts once (#549).
- **Bills from a Google Drive folder (#550).** An owner or admin connects Google Drive, read-only, and chooses one
  folder. Each PDF, `.eml` or `.txt` file added to it from then on is read every 15 minutes, or at once with **Check
  Google Drive now**, and waits in the review list on Bills & receivables. A person adds or dismisses each one: a file
  in the folder is never by itself a reason to pay. Files already in the folder when it is chosen are never read.
- **Everything waiting, on the accountant console (#551).** `/accountant` now also lists bills waiting for a verdict in
  shadow mode, invoices to review, contractor work to accept, and last month, until its close pack is downloaded or
  exported.
- **A fifth free tool: the payout batch check (#552)**, at [/check-payouts](https://www.vestiarion.xyz/check-payouts).
  Paste a batch payout or drop its file, such as Safe's CSV Airdrop or a Disperse list.
  - Every address gets the address checker's rules, OFAC's list included, and is compared with every other address in
    the batch, so two made to look alike are both a stop, unless a batch that long holds such a pair by chance, when
    both are a look.
  - Amounts are checked, each token's total is added up exactly, and a token contract made to look like USDC is a stop.
  - It runs in the browser: nothing pasted is sent anywhere or kept.
- **For builders (#545, #553, #554, #555, #556, #557, #558).**
  - **MCP (#545).** The MCP server has five new read-only tools for a bill's or a milestone's evidence, a receipt, the
    payee lock's evidence and a month's close pack. Each answers a digest of the signed file that says the text in it
    came from outside and is data, never instructions. The server now lists 18 tools.
  - **CLI 0.2.0 (#553).** `vestiarion evidence export` and `vestiarion close-pack export` save the signed files, and
    `vestiarion evidence verify` and `vestiarion close-pack verify` check them offline, answering VALID, BROKEN or NOT
    CHECKED.
  - **n8n nodes (#554 to #558).** `@vestiarion/n8n-nodes-vestiarion` adds a node that creates bills and reads bills,
    counterparties, decisions and the workspace's status, and a trigger that checks each webhook delivery's signature.
    It never approves or pays. Versions from 0.1.1 are published from GitHub Actions with npm provenance; 0.1.3 follows
    n8n's first review and is in n8n's verification review. Until n8n verifies it, it installs on self-hosted n8n
    only, since n8n Cloud lists only verified nodes. The
    [n8n guide](https://www.vestiarion.xyz/docs/get-started/n8n) opens with a walkthrough video.
- **Production checked about every 10 minutes (#540).** From GitHub's servers, a check opens the landing page, the
  open numbers, the docs, the five free tools, robots.txt and the sitemap, and tells the team on a private Discord
  channel when one fails two rounds running. Each run checks every 10 minutes for almost 6 hours, and GitHub starts
  the hourly runs late, so the checks have paused between runs, for up to about 3 hours.

## What changed

What a workspace notices without turning anything on:
- The audit log and Insights pages send far less to open: measured on a hundred audit entries, the list went from
  about 1.8 MB to 0.4 MB, and Insights with the longest history it reads from about 2.3 MB to 0.8 MB. What they show
  is unchanged, except that an audit row opened before the page is ready shows an empty panel until it is (#547).
- The OFAC vendor check says that only 0x wallets are compared with each other (#539).
- The bill list check runs in a background tab too, after about a second (#542).
- The Quality Lab's replay covers 55 of the team's shadow-mode decisions: 46 reach the same outcome through today's code
  and 9 cannot be rebuilt from their record, with 0 wrong payments (#559). The replay runs the decision code outside
  shadow mode, so it does not test the earned-vendor gate: the scenarios do.

For integrators and self-hosted deployments:
- `GET /api/v1/milestones` can return `linear` as a milestone's `verificationMethod`, and the TypeScript SDK 0.4.1, on
  npm, types the value. A milestone is not linked to a Linear issue through the API, and the write API still adds a
  milestone pending.
- The MCP server has five new tools (above). Its `server.json` and version are unchanged.
- The ledger:
  - new entries `earned_vendor_rule_changed`, `vendor_earned`, `vendor_earned_lost` and
    `earned_vendor_payments_changed`;
  - for Linear, `linear_issue_linked`, `linear_issue_unlinked`, `linear_issue_ready`, `linear_work_accepted` and
    `linear_acceptance_withdrawn`;
  - for Google Drive, `google_drive_folder_chosen`, `invoice_drive_file_received` and `invoice_drive_file_dismissed`,
    and `create_invoice` with `via: "google_drive"`;
  - `ap_pay` gains `earned`, and `approval_paid` gains `recorded`, `approvedTogether` and `batch`;
  - `weekly_brief_sent` gains `paid.onItsOwn`, `vendor_earned_lost` can carry `trigger: "cycle"`, and
    `shadow_mode_started`'s summary names the exception for earned vendors;
  - `milestone_criteria_set` is recorded for a milestone linked to Linear, and `accounting_connected` can name
    `provider: "linear"` or `provider: "googledrive"`.

  The [API changelog](https://www.vestiarion.xyz/docs/changelog) lists each change in six entries dated Oct 28 to
  Nov 1.
- New settings: `LINEAR_CLIENT_ID`, `LINEAR_CLIENT_SECRET` and `LINEAR_WEBHOOK_SECRET` for Linear;
  `GOOGLE_DRIVE_CLIENT_ID` and `GOOGLE_DRIVE_CLIENT_SECRET` for Google Drive, from its own Google client. The
  production check needs the GitHub secret `MONITOR_DISCORD_WEBHOOK_URL` to send alerts.
- Packages: SDK 0.4.1, CLI 0.2.0 (which needs SDK 0.4.1), and the n8n nodes at 0.1.3.
- Apply the migrations below before upgrading.

## Known limitations

- **Beta.** Screens, wording and features change often. The spending-limit contracts and the escrow contract have not
  been audited. Their source is verified, which is not an audit.
- **Not every change was reviewed by an independent reviewer.** 5 of the 22 pull requests, listed above, record no
  independent review before merge.
- **No customer has paid a real bill on Arc mainnet yet.**
- **Earned vendors** are shadow mode and Arc testnet only. Two syncs at the same moment can sign the same status twice,
  and a Disagree given while a cycle runs takes effect at its next stage. The cash priority plan does not run in shadow
  mode.
- **Approve many:** the operating wallet must hold the total, since a selection never brings cash back from the
  reserve. If a send fails while a batch is claimed, its bills stay submitting for about 15 minutes. Slack still
  approves one bill at a time; Telegram, Discord and the API approve none.
- **Linear:** if Linear cannot be read when Vestiarion checks an accepted issue, the acceptance stands as the person
  gave it. Linear's logo is not shown yet, and who completed the issue is not recorded: the card's "completed by" and
  the ledger entries leave it empty.
- **Google Drive:** until Google verifies the read-only Drive permission, Google shows its "Google hasn't verified this
  app" screen, and at most 100 people can grant it. Pictures, scans, Google Docs files, shortcuts, files over 4 MB and
  subfolders are not read, and a folder with more than 1,000 files is refused.
- **The accountant console** does not list bills brought in from Xero, QuickBooks or Zoho Books yet.
- **The CLI:** rerunning an export whose default file exists spends the read before refusing.
- **The n8n nodes:** until n8n verifies them, they install on self-hosted n8n only. A workflow saved with 0.1.2 needs
  its moved fields set again in 0.1.3. There is no polling trigger yet, and the webhook endpoint is added by hand in
  Settings.
- **The production check** has no daily heartbeat, so a quiet channel cannot tell all checks passing from no run
  starting, and its runs have paused for up to about 3 hours when GitHub started one late.
- **The free tools are a first look.**
  - The OFAC checks cover the SDN list only, as of its snapshot's date. They cannot see who owns a company, and do not
    compare a name written in a script other than Latin letters.
  - The payout batch check does not look up ENS names or check addresses that are not EVM ones.
  - The bill list check does not know which bills were paid, and the calculator does not know the local rules on late
    fees.
- **PDFs that can be measured are still read without a time limit,** as in v0.1.0-beta.14. This applies to PDFs read
  from a document, an email, a Google Drive folder, the Chrome extension, Telegram, Slack or the payee link.
- **Vendor documents:** an image, a scan, a PDF over 4 MB or of more than 50 pages, or a password-protected one is
  not checked. A goods receipt is never marked from a document.
- **Checks that v0.1.0-beta.14 listed as undone, with none recorded as done since:** posting the waiting list, with its **Review** links, to a real Slack
  workspace (#528); following a workspace credited to a free tool through sign-in in a real browser (#533); and
  whether the project's X account accepts direct messages from accounts it does not follow (#531).
- **The Quality Lab tests the decision code, not the model's judgement.** It covers USDC bills on Arc only, and 9 of
  the 55 replayed decisions cannot be rebuilt from their record, so they are run both ways instead. The replay does
  not test the earned-vendor gate.
- **QuickBooks Online** still connects to sandbox companies only, until Intuit's app assessment is complete.
- **Bank payouts** run on BlindPay's development instance, to vendors in Mexico over SPEI only.
