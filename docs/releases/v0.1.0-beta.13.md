# Vestiarion v0.1.0-beta.13 — Evidence for your accountant, vendor documents and late fees

The thirteenth beta of Vestiarion. This release brings the people around a small business's bills into the workspace.
An accountant can co-sign larger bills, sees what waits for them, and can invite a client with one link. A vendor can
be asked for a bill's missing purchase order or goods receipt, and its statement of account can be checked against
the books. A bill can carry the late fee its supplier charges, so waiting shows what it costs. Also in this release:
- the payee lock's evidence pack, for an insurer or an accountant, and a payee's own contact asked "Was this you?" when
  its address changes;
- a payout address on OFAC's sanctions list is never paid, and an invoice that asks to be paid elsewhere is held;
- the approval card and the receipt say what a payment costs and who paid each fee;
- Arc mainnet pays vendors on Arbitrum, as well as on Base;
- bills waiting for a person are posted to Discord and Telegram, each with a link to review it;
- every page enforces a Content Security Policy.

## Highlights

- **Your accountant, in the workspace (#503, #504, #505, #507, #513).**
  - **Co-sign (#504).** In **Settings**, under **Two approvals**, an owner can choose **Let accountants co-sign**. An
    accountant's **Co-sign** on a bill above the two-approvals figure records one of the two approvals and never pays,
    so the approval that pays is always an owner's, admin's or approver's. A founder alone with their accountant can
    then use two approvals. It is off by default, and milestones are not co-signed.
  - **What waits for them (#507).** The accountant console lists each bill waiting for their co-sign and each changed
    address no one has confirmed. A bill that cannot be co-signed says why, in place of a button that would be refused.
    The month's hand-off lists co-signs among the decisions.
  - **Invite a client with one link (#513).** On the accountant console, under **Invite a client**, an accountant makes
    a one-time link that works for 7 days. The client opens it signed in, names a workspace and gives consent. The
    workspace opens as theirs, on Arc testnet in shadow mode, with the accountant already in it. Only the link's
    SHA-256 is kept. Going live offers to bring the accountant along.
  - **Check a vendor's statement (#503, #505).** On **Counterparties**, **Check their statement** reads a vendor's
    statement of account, pasted or as a CSV, and matches each line to your bills and your payments. It lists:
    - what is missing here, with an **Add** for each;
    - what is listed twice, and amounts that differ;
    - payments of yours they have not credited, with the transaction to send them;
    - payments they list that you have no record of;
    - both balances.

    Nothing is paid or changed.
- **Vendors send what a bill is missing (#512).**
  - In **Settings**, beside the payee lock, an owner sets **Ask vendors for missing documents** to off (the default),
    shadow or on.
  - On, when a bill waits for its purchase order or goods receipt and its vendor has a billing email, Vestiarion
    emails the vendor a one-time link in the workspace's name. Without an account, the vendor sends one PDF, PNG or
    JPEG of up to 10 MB, and may type the purchase order number and a note.
  - The file is kept in the workspace's private storage, and the bill's card, on Approvals and on Bills, shows what
    arrived.
  - Nothing is paid because a document arrived: a person adds the details, and the agent decides the bill again.
  - In shadow, nothing is sent, and each card says what Vestiarion would ask.
- **Late fees (#514).** A bill can carry the late fee its supplier charges: a percent a month, or a fixed amount, from
  the day it starts. It is entered in the invoice form, through the API or in an imported list, or read from a
  document, an email, the Chrome extension or a chat. A late fee is never added to what is paid; it shows what waiting
  costs:
  - the approval card's cost box ends with "Late after *date*" and what a month of the fee costs;
  - past its due date, a bill names the fee beside its decide-by day, in Approvals, on Bills & receivables and in the
    accountant console;
  - past the due date, one more escalation email comes on the eve of the fee, while paying still avoids it;
  - when cash is short, the written policy pays first, among the bills due or overdue, the one whose fee costs most in
    the coming month.
- **Proof of how each changed address was checked (#474, #479, #502).**
  - Any member opens the payee lock's evidence pack from **Settings**, under **Payee lock**, for a period they choose.
  - It shows one row per payout address change: who entered the address, how it was checked and whether by a second
    person, the lock's wait, the payments the agent held for it, and its first payment.
  - It answers, from those records, the questions insurers ask about changed payment details.
  - It prints, saves as a PDF, downloads as CSV, and downloads as a JSON file carrying every ledger entry it cites, to
    check offline.
  - A new guide, [Share the evidence pack and receipts](https://www.vestiarion.xyz/docs/guides/evidence-and-receipts),
    walks an owner or their accountant through it.
- **Fewer ways to pay the wrong address (#475, #477, #483, #488, #492, #493, #494).**
  - **Sanctioned addresses (#475).** Every cycle checks each counterparty's payout address against the addresses on
    OFAC's SDN list, read every 12 hours in production. A listed address makes the counterparty high risk, and the
    agent, **Approve and pay** and **Pay now** refuse to pay it.
  - **An invoice that asks to be paid elsewhere (#483).** When an invoice read from a document asks to be paid to an
    address that is not the counterparty's address on file, the agent holds the payment for a person, naming both
    addresses. **Approve and pay** still pays only the address on file.
  - **"Was this you?" (#488).** When a payee's address changes, its billing email on file is asked, through a one-time
    link, whether they made the change. "No, this wasn't me" freezes the payee: no one can confirm its address and no
    approval pays it until an owner or admin checks with them and unfreezes it, saying how.
  - **The payee lock starts on for a new Arc mainnet workspace (#492).** Workspaces already on Arc mainnet keep their
    setting.
  - **ENS names and Basenames (#493).** Type `northwind.eth` or `northwind.base.eth` in a payee's address field to see
    the address it points to. Only the address is saved.
  - **The weekly brief (#494)** has a **Payee addresses** section in a week when a payee's address changed.
  - **A free address lookalike checker (#477)** at [/check-address](https://www.vestiarion.xyz/check-address). It runs
    in the browser: nothing pasted is sent anywhere.
- **What a payment costs, before and after (#495, #496, #499, #500).**
  - The approval card shows the invoice, an early-payment discount that still applies, the fee for a payee on another
    chain, what leaves the wallet in all, and what the payee receives (#496).
  - A bank payout's card compares it with USDC sent to a wallet, where no fee is taken (#500).
  - A payee link that lets a vendor choose a network says what each one costs the business (#499).
  - A shared receipt lists the invoice and its number, the discount, the route's fee and who paid it, and what the
    payee received, read from their chain. It saves as a PDF (#495).
- **Pay a vendor on Arbitrum from Arc mainnet (#498, #501).**
  - Arbitrum joins Base as a chain Arc mainnet pays across to through CCTP, in real USDC. Circle quoted about
    0.077 USDC to forward a payout there on Oct 22, paid on top of the invoice.
  - The TypeScript SDK 0.3.6 accepts `ARB`.
  - Proven on Oct 22 from the team's own test workspace, for a 1 USDC bill the workspace's only approver approved:
    - [burn on Arc](https://explorer.arc.io/tx/0xce0a7911791ba04b6ff79c2dee4cdfc213f7fd914c5a29d6b58ae5a4b19bd7b8);
    - [1 USDC minted on Arbitrum](https://arbiscan.io/tx/0x385363d4912e5ddce757984a789a39bf983d6a7177a943add36d7e604fadf987).
- **Bills that wait for you, where you already talk (#508, #509, #511, #515).**
  - **Discord (#508, #509).** An owner or admin pastes a channel webhook's URL in **Settings**. After each cycle, the
    bills held, flagged or waiting for details are posted to that channel, each with a link to review it. Vestiarion
    reads no message in Discord.
  - **Telegram (#511).** The message after each cycle ends with the bills still waiting, each with **Review**.
  - **Why code stopped a bill (#515).** In those messages and in `/waiting`, a bill a check in code stopped now says
    what stopped it, in the words of its row on Bills & receivables, instead of the agent's first sentence.
  - Opening a link never approves anything.
- **Nothing waits for the six-hourly schedule (#471, #472).**
  - A watch runs every 5 minutes. When the payee lock's wait ends on a held payment, the payment is decided within
    about five minutes, and a scheduled cycle that was missed runs within about 20 minutes. On Oct 20, in the team's
    own workspace on Arc testnet, it reopened a held bill 4 minutes after its wait ended.
  - A payment whose first send never reached Circle, and that was not sent again while something held it, is decided
    again once that ends. It is paid on the same payment record, under the same key, so it is never paid twice (#472).
- **Security you can check (#476, #478, #480, #484, #490, #506, #510).**
  - Every page enforces a Content Security Policy. Only where forms may go is still report-only (#476, #478, #490).
  - `main` is a protected branch, and the security page says so (#480).
  - Every deployed contract's source is verified on Sourcify, an exact match, and shows on Blockscout.
    [Contracts on Arc](https://www.vestiarion.xyz/docs/contracts#verified-source) links each one (#510).
  - A deployment chooses its screening service: OpenSanctions' hosted API, or a self-hosted yente server (#484).
  - Workspace status says **Live, last lookup failed** when a live workspace's newest screening lookup failed, and the
    platform team can check the production key from `/admin/screening` (#506).
- **Smaller changes (#473, #481, #482, #485, #486, #487, #489, #491, #497).**
  - Approvals and Contractors work on a phone: the whole address, and buttons a thumb's size (#487).
  - The header says **Test payments live** on Arc testnet and **Real payments live** on Arc mainnet (#482).
  - **Delete account** moved out of the account menu, into **Settings** under **Danger zone** and a new **Your
    account** page (#485).
  - Settings' contents scroll on a short window (#481).
  - Bills & receivables asks for its data at once instead of one read after another: 6 rounds of reads instead of 18
    in its test (#497).
  - The README shows the payee lock's two Arc testnet payments, links the full product tour, and shows the SDK's npm
    version (#473, #486, #489). The founder dashboard shows retention by week (#491).

## What changed

What a workspace notices without turning anything on:
- A payout address on OFAC's SDN list is refused.
- A payment is held for a person when its invoice, read from a document, asks to be paid to an address not on file.
- In a live workspace, a payee with a billing email is asked "Was this you?" when its address changes to a new one.
  Nothing is asked for a first address, a sandbox, shadow mode or a payee with no billing email.
- A new Arc mainnet workspace starts with the payee lock on, with no wait.
- Pages enforce a Content Security Policy. The reports production sent while it was report-only showed nothing of the
  site's own blocked, only browser extensions' attempts to run code from text.
- The Telegram message after each cycle lists the bills still waiting, and no longer repeats them among the decisions.
- The header names test or real payments.
- **Delete account** is in **Settings** and **Your account**, no longer in the account menu.

For integrators and self-hosted deployments:
- `/api/v1` accepts `chain: "ARB"` for a vendor on Arc mainnet and `lateFee` on an invoice, and the ledger has new
  entries and fields for this release's features, from "Was this you?" to client links. The
  [API changelog](https://www.vestiarion.xyz/docs/changelog) lists each, in 15 entries dated Oct 21 to Oct 23.
- The TypeScript SDK 0.3.6 (`ARB`) and 0.3.7 (`lateFee`) are on npm. The CLI stays at 0.1.0.
- `SCREENING_PROVIDER` names the screening service. A service named without its key or address stops the app at
  start; a deployment that sets nothing new screens as before. `npm run screening:check` screens one name with a new
  key before it is set, and never prints the key.
- Apply the migrations below before upgrading.

## Known limitations

- **Beta.** Screens, wording and features change often. The spending-limit contracts (versions 1 and 2) and the
  escrow contract have not been audited. Their source is now verified, which is not an audit.
- **Not every change was reviewed by an independent reviewer.** 23 of the 45 pull requests, listed above, record no
  independent review before merge, the Arbitrum payouts (#498) among them.
- **No customer has paid a real bill on Arc mainnet yet.** The Arc mainnet payouts to Base and Arbitrum were made from
  the team's own test workspaces.
- **Across chains from Arc mainnet: Base and Arbitrum, USDC only.** A spending-limit contract deployed from an owner's
  wallet before Oct 22 pays Base only, and Vestiarion offers only the chains it pays.
- **Late fees are shown, never paid.** A fixed fee in your own currency is refused in shadow mode: give it as a
  percent a month.
- **Vendor document requests** are off by default, ask only for a purchase order or a goods receipt, and never pay a
  bill on their own.
- **Accountants' co-signing** is off by default and does not cover milestones. A client link opens a workspace on Arc
  testnet in shadow mode only.
- **The Content Security Policy** allows inline scripts, since pages carry no nonce, and where forms may go is still
  report-only.
- **Bank payouts** run on BlindPay's development instance, to vendors in Mexico over SPEI only.
- **QuickBooks Online** still connects to sandbox companies only, until Intuit's app assessment is complete.
- **Two checks the pull requests left undone:** Bills & receivables' load time in production after #497, and
  comparing the new guide (#502) with the live pages in a browser.
