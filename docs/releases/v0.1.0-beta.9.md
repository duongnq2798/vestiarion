# Vestiarion v0.1.0-beta.9 — The agent works through a cash shortfall

The ninth beta of Vestiarion. This batch is about the days when the cash is short. The agent now chooses which bills
get the cash first and says why, asks suppliers for more time and answers their counters, and offers customers a
discount to pay sooner. It checks scheduled payments against the cash ahead, and tells the owner before the cash runs
short. Every Monday it sends a brief of the week, and businesses in shadow mode get one about their verdicts. The main
guides open with a short video.

This release covers 22 merged pull requests (#409 to #430) since
[v0.1.0-beta.8](https://github.com/duongnq2798/vestiarion/releases/tag/v0.1.0-beta.8). Not every change in this
batch was reviewed by an independent reviewer before merge. The five largest were (#412, #418, #420, #427, #429), and
those reviews found 33 defects, of which 31 were fixed before merge. One was documented as a limitation, and one, an
index, waits for a migration. Each change passed CI, and each feature was tested end to end on the live product,
where the live tests found the defects fixed in #415, #416, #417, #419, #422 and #430. Production is deployed from
`main` on every merge, so it may already run newer changes than this release lists.

## Highlights

- **The agent chooses which bills to pay first (#429, #430).**
  - When the bills due within 14 days add up to more than the cash, the model orders them by what costs the business
    most to pay late, saying why for each. The written policy's date order is its reference and its fallback.
  - Code keeps bills in that order only while the cash lasts, so the agent never keeps more than the wallet holds. It
    holds the rest for a person, even a bill the model would pay.
  - The choice is signed once as `cash_priority_set`, and reused with no model call while the bills stay the same.
  - Proven on Arc testnet, with 57.03 USDC and two bills due the same day: the written policy would have paid a 30 USDC
    one-off archive migration and left no room for the 50 USDC database backups. The agent paid the backups and held
    the migration: "The production database backups stop if this 50 USDC monthly renewal on Oct 17, 2026 is unpaid,
    so it costs the most to delay." Earlier, with 147.03 USDC, it kept 45 USDC hosting and storage bills over a
    140 USDC one-off render job the policy would have paid first.
  - The live test found the model misreading a supplier's history: it read a request the agent withdrew as the
    supplier's yes, then a counter the agent declined as its no. #430 tells it how each request ended.
- **Requests for more time (#411, #413, #414, #415, #417, #419).**
  - When the operating wallet cannot pay a bill due within two weeks, the agent emails the supplier a one-time link
    asking for the same amount on a later day. An owner or admin turns it on, with the most days it may ask.
  - The supplier agrees, declines, or proposes fewer days. The agent answers a counter itself: it accepts only a date
    the business can keep, with what it holds and what customers owe by then.
  - The agent withdraws a request it no longer needs, when money comes in or the bill is no longer held, and tells the
    supplier.
  - Code works out when a bill is covered and gives the model that. Only a supplier's own decline pauses further
    requests to it, and the model is told which requests the agent took back.
  - Proven: the agent asked for 5 more days on a 150 USDC bill, the supplier proposed 3, and the agent declined, since
    the bill was not covered by then. A request on a 140 USDC bill was withdrawn, and the supplier told, once cash came
    in.
- **Customer discounts while cash is short (#412).** When bills are held for want of cash, the agent can offer customers
  a small discount, within the most an owner or admin sets, to pay an open invoice within 5 days. Proven: a customer
  accepted and paid 29.925 USDC on a 30 USDC invoice, and the agent matched it at the discount.
- **Scheduled payments checked against the cash ahead (#418).** Each cycle the agent measures every USDC bill scheduled
  for a later day, and decides again now one the cash will not cover then. That gives it days to ask for more time or
  offer discounts. Proven: two 45 USDC bills set for Oct 24 and 25 were held a week early.
- **Told before the cash runs short (#420, #421).** A new cycle stage, `cash_alert`, says once in the console,
  Telegram and Slack how much is needed and by when, what customers owe by then, and what the agent is already doing.
  It says it again only when that changes, and says when the cash covers everything again. The amount is rounded up to
  the cent.
- **The weekly brief (#427, #428).**
  - On Mondays, owners, admins and approvers get the week by email: what was paid and received, the decisions, what
    waits for them, what leaves next, and whether the cash covers the next 30 days.
  - In shadow mode, the brief reports the verdicts instead, and how often the business agreed with the agent.
  - **Send me this week's summary**, in Settings, sends it at once. Both were proven by email.
- **A short video for each main flow (#423, #425, #426).** Twelve guides open with a narrated walkthrough of two
  minutes or less, recorded in the live product, with a transcript. The demo and product tour are voiced again, and
  every video is linked on YouTube.
- **The landing page's proof says which rule decides (#410).** Rules in code decide; the written policy is kept only
  to compare. The FAQ says how long it takes to start and what it costs.
- **Fixes.**
  - Receipts and evidence pages check Arc testnet again, at its new RPC address (#422).
  - Leaving Bills & receivables no longer asks about a bill already added (#416).
  - The live provider tests no longer reach the network (#424).
- **Release notes (#409)** for v0.1.0-beta.8.

## Known limitations

- **Beta.** Screens, wording and features change often. The spending-limit and escrow contracts have not been audited.
- **Cash priority** covers USDC bills only, not bills paid to a bank account, and does not run in shadow mode. A bill
  waiting for cash that a guardrail would flag is held for cash first, and flagged when it is decided again.
- **Requests for more time:** accepting a supplier's counter has not been tested live yet.
- **The cash alert** reads the ledger without an index for its action; one is planned with the next migration.
- **Bank payouts** run on BlindPay's development instance. Only vendors in Mexico, over SPEI.
- **QuickBooks Online** still connects to sandbox companies only, until Intuit's app assessment is complete.
