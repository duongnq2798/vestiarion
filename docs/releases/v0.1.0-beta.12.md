# Vestiarion v0.1.0-beta.12 — The payee lock

The twelfth beta of Vestiarion. The costliest fraud a small business meets is a payee's payout details being changed
to the fraudster's. This release adds a **payee lock**: when it is on, a changed payout address is paid only after a
second person records how they checked it, and optionally only after a wait. Also in this release:
- payees send their invoice, and choose where they are paid, through their payee link;
- the app's functions now run beside the database, so pages and actions answer several times sooner;
- the landing page now follows one recorded bill from start to finish;
- the public pages share one visual system.

This release covers 14 merged pull requests (#456 to #469) since
[v0.1.0-beta.11](https://github.com/duongnq2798/vestiarion/releases/tag/v0.1.0-beta.11). Each change passed CI.
- **Reviewed by an independent reviewer before merge:** #457, #458, #459, #461, #462, #463, #464, #466 and #468.
- **Not reviewed by an independent reviewer before merge, as far as the pull requests record:** #456 (the beta.11 notes), #460, #465, #467 and #469.

Production deploys from `main` on every merge, so it may already run changes newer than this list.

## Highlights

- **The payee lock (#466, #467, #468).**
  - An owner turns it on in **Settings**, under **Payee lock**. It is off by default, so nothing changes for a
    workspace that leaves it off.
  - **Confirm address** now asks how the address was checked: a call or a message to a contact held before the
    change, in person, or another way, with the contact used and a note. The record is signed with the confirmation.
  - With the lock on:
    - the record is required;
    - whoever entered or asked for the address cannot confirm it while another member can;
    - approving a payment no longer confirms its address;
    - an optional wait of 4 to 72 hours keeps a changed address from being paid, confirmed or not. The agent holds a
      payable as `counterparty.cooling_off` and decides it again when the wait ends. A verified milestone waits too,
      and its card on **Contractors** reads "Payee lock wait".
  - With the lock on or off, Counterparties warns about an address that matches or looks like another payee's, or
    like one the same payee had before, which is how address poisoning works.
  - Checked live in the team's own Arc mainnet workspace:
    - turning the lock on was signed;
    - a weak record was refused;
    - a confirmation with a record was signed with its 24-hour wait.

  No money moved in that check.
- **A payee sends its invoice through its link (#458, #460, #462).**
  - A payee link can also ask for the payee's invoice: number, amount in USDC, due date, and the file's text and
    SHA-256. The invoice lands in **Bills → Waiting for review**.
  - It can also let a vendor with no address choose where it is paid: Arc mainnet or Base, or Arc testnet or a
    Sepolia chain.
  - The page moves to its next step as soon as an answer is accepted, and names the payee's chain.
- **Pages and actions answer sooner (#463).**
  - The app's functions moved from Washington to Tokyo, beside the database.
  - Measured on production, time to the last byte, before → after:
    - Bills: 6.3 s → 1.0 s;
    - Treasury: 4.4 s → 0.7 s;
    - Counterparties: 3.4 s → 0.5 s;
    - a payee link's step: 1.7 s → 0.4 s;
    - creating a payee link: 3 to 7 s → 1.1 s.
- **A landing page that follows one bill (#457, #459, #461).**
  - It follows a bill from the team's own workspace through the agent's checks, the coded hold, a person's approval
    and the payment, with signed ledger entries and explorer links.
  - The hero names the decisions it shows.
  - It rests on a shared visual system.
- **Public pages in the same system (#465, #469).** The payee, supplier, payment, receipt, statement and sign-in pages
  share the landing's look and have larger touch areas on phones. The sign-in card ends at its button.
- **A fix (#464).** "Pay early for a discount" was hidden, with an error in the logs, in workspaces without a
  reserve. It shows again.

## Known limitations

- **Beta.** Screens, wording and features change often. The spending-limit contracts (versions 1 and 2) and the
  escrow contract have not been audited.
- **The payee lock covers addresses on Arc and the chains it pays.** A bank payout already waits for a person every
  time, so the lock adds nothing there. It has been checked live in the team's own workspace only. No customer has
  turned it on yet.
- **Base only, USDC only, across chains on Arc mainnet.**
- **No customer has paid a real bill on Arc mainnet yet.** The Arc mainnet payments so far are the team's.
- **Bank payouts** run on BlindPay's development instance, to vendors in Mexico over SPEI only.
- **QuickBooks Online** still connects to sandbox companies only, until Intuit's app assessment is complete.
