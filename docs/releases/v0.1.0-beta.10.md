# Vestiarion v0.1.0-beta.10 — From shadow mode to the first real payment

The tenth beta of Vestiarion. This batch is about the people around the agent. Suppliers can see when they will be paid
without asking. A person can change which bills the cash goes to first, and the agent keeps that order. A business that
has watched the agent in shadow mode can open its first real-money workspace on Arc mainnet in one step, with its
suppliers carried over. The scheduled cycles reach every live workspace again.

This release covers 10 merged pull requests (#431 to #440) since
[v0.1.0-beta.9](https://github.com/duongnq2798/vestiarion/releases/tag/v0.1.0-beta.9). The three features (#436, #437,
#440) were each reviewed by an independent reviewer before merge. Those reviews found 23 defects, and all of them were
fixed before merge. The smaller changes were not reviewed independently. Each change passed CI, and each feature was
tested end to end on the live product, where the live test of #437 found the issues fixed in #439. Production is
deployed from `main` on every merge, so it may already run newer changes than this release lists.

## Highlights

- **Start the live slice (#440).**
  - On the report of a workspace in shadow mode, under **Before a live slice**, an owner ticks the vendors to pay in
    real USDC and opens a workspace on Arc mainnet with them. Each vendor carries its name, payment limit, notice email
    and purchase-order setting, so nothing is typed in again.
  - A vendor's own confirmed Arc address is carried and waits for a person to confirm it again there. A mirror address,
    an address on another chain and one not yet confirmed are never carried: the vendor adds its own from a payee link.
  - No bill and no money moves. The new workspace goes live from its own Settings, and the report's last step ticks
    once it is live. Signed as `live_slice_started`.
  - Proven in production: a shadow workspace opened its live slice on Arc mainnet with two vendors, one own address
    waiting for confirmation and one mirror address left behind.
- **A person's order for which bill first (#437, #439).**
  - The **Payment plan** tab shows the agent's order under **Which bill first**, with each bill's reason and what the
    written policy would pay first. An owner, admin or approver moves bills up or down and saves.
  - The agent keeps that order with no model call while the bills stay the same, and keeps it for the bills still due
    once some are paid. It never pays more than the cash holds. Whoever entered a bill cannot move it ahead on their
    own, unless they are the workspace's only approver.
  - Proven on Arc testnet: a member put a 45 USDC storage bill first, and once 80 test USDC came in, the next cycle
    reopened both 45 USDC bills in that order and scheduled them for their due days.
  - The live test found the reasons naming bills by an internal ref and in dollars, and the model reading the agent's
    own requests for more time as the supplier's offers. #439 fixed both, and closed a maker-checker gap when the cash
    covers no bill.
- **A supplier's payment status (#436).**
  - An owner or admin sends a vendor a link to its own bills: where each one stands (received, being reviewed, the
    day it is set to be paid, on its way, or paid), what is still to be paid in each currency, and each payment's
    transaction on Arc.
  - The link needs no account. It never says why a bill is held and shows nothing else of the workspace. It works for
    7, 30 or 90 days and can be stopped at any time. In shadow mode it shows no bills.
- **Every live workspace gets its scheduled cycle again (#433).** The six-hourly cycles ran one workspace after another
  and, with twelve live workspaces, outran their five minutes, so the last workspaces by name got no cycle. Four now run
  at once, and a cycle that cannot finish in time is left to the next run. All twelve were measured finishing in about
  100 seconds.
- **Docs and README.** The README and ARCHITECTURE describe the Oct 17 batch (#431). The README links the new demo and
  full tour (#432, #434, #435), and the Xero and HubSpot guides link their videos on YouTube (#438).

## Known limitations

- **Beta.** Screens, wording and features change often. The spending-limit and escrow contracts have not been audited.
- **The live slice** opens the workspace and carries the vendors. No shadow-mode business has made its first real
  payment on Arc mainnet through it yet. Two owners pressing at the same moment can open two slices; the report shows
  the newer one.
- **A supplier's payment status** is for vendors only, not contractors or customers.
- **Cash priority** covers USDC bills only, not bills paid to a bank account, and does not run in shadow mode.
- **The cash alert** still reads the ledger without an index for its action.
- **Bank payouts** run on BlindPay's development instance. Only vendors in Mexico, over SPEI.
- **QuickBooks Online** still connects to sandbox companies only, until Intuit's app assessment is complete.
