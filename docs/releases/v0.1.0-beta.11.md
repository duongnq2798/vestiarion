# Vestiarion v0.1.0-beta.11 — Pay contractors on Base from Arc mainnet

The eleventh beta of Vestiarion. A business on Arc mainnet can now pay a vendor's USDC address on Base, where most
contractors who want USDC already hold it. It works from the workspace's own Circle account, and from the owner's own
wallet through a new version of the spending-limit contract. Both routes were proven with real USDC on Oct 19, 2026.
The rest of the batch is about bringing people in and keeping them:
- a one-time reminder to a workspace that stops before its first bill or first verdict;
- a prompt to bring in an approver and the accountant;
- one step to ask every payee for an address;
- pages and emails a counterparty gets now say who sent them;
- a rewritten landing page.

This release covers 15 merged pull requests (#441 to #455) since
[v0.1.0-beta.10](https://github.com/duongnq2798/vestiarion/releases/tag/v0.1.0-beta.10). Each change passed CI. The
payment changes (#453, #454) were reviewed by an independent reviewer before merge and then tested with real USDC on
Arc mainnet. The review of #453 also found and fixed a defect that predates it: a milestone for a vendor on another
chain would have been paid on Arc. Production is deployed from `main` on every merge, so it may already run newer
changes than this release lists.

## Highlights

- **Pay a vendor on Base from Arc mainnet (#453).**
  - Arc mainnet now lists Base as a chain a vendor can be paid on, through Circle's CCTP V2 with its Forwarding
    Service. The operating wallet burns the USDC on Arc, Circle mints it to the vendor on Base, and the vendor needs no
    gas there.
  - The fee (about 0.055 USDC when tested) is read before the agent decides and again before the burn, paid on top of
    the invoice, and held for a person above 10% of the bill. A payment counts as paid only once it is minted on Base.
  - This route runs from a workspace on its own Circle account.
  - A milestone is never paid across chains: a vendor on another chain cannot be given one, and one made earlier is
    held.
  - A live slice carries a vendor on Base Sepolia over as a vendor on Base, without the address.
  - TypeScript SDK 0.3.5 accepts `BASE`.
  - Proven on Arc mainnet:
    - [burn on Arc](https://explorer.arc.io/tx/0x89f66f7b618915e4e147c92dd175ba54a3fad8f8f5c689b616f3e552601617e9);
    - [1 USDC minted on Base](https://basescan.org/tx/0x33b0f0e5ebd3422a999bfd31ad913033d6396b54a9817e9c7eba1621ee7df0ac).
- **Pay a vendor on Base from your own wallet (#454).**
  - Version 2 of the spending-limit contract adds `payAcrossChains`. The agent's wallet, which holds only gas, asks
    the contract to pay. The contract takes the payment and its CCTP fee from the owner's wallet, counts both against
    its daily and 7-day figures, and burns them for Base, so the agent never holds the owner's USDC.
  - The contract itself refuses a fee above 10% of the payout or above 2 USDC, and any chain but Base.
  - Every new setup deploys version 2. A workspace on version 1 upgrades from **Go live**, in one passkey confirmation
    or three wallet steps.
  - Proven on Arc mainnet from a passkey wallet, in a workspace that began in shadow mode:
    - [`payAcrossChains` on Arc](https://explorer.arc.io/tx/0x7c3b4b6b2561ca3d3dc0cd15594d7e0e8a9fd6717113e996a14acb243e642a17);
    - [1 USDC minted on Base](https://basescan.org/tx/0x9ea0fe5aa1b57f799595a48f336d1e008971e9a1d067dac1707ce02ff656150c).
  - In both tests the agent read the live fee and chose to pay on the due date, the first-payment guardrail held the
    bill, and the workspace's only approver approved it.
- **A reminder when a workspace stops (#452).**
  - A customer workspace with no real bill a day after it was opened gets one email with the ways to give the agent a
    bill.
  - A workspace in shadow mode whose agent's decisions still wait for a first verdict three days on gets one email
    saying how many wait.
  - Each email goes once per workspace. The agent tick sends them, since its scheduled cycles reach only live
    workspaces.
  - The founder dashboard counts the workspaces reminded and how many came back.
  - The first two went out on Oct 19.
- **Bring in a second person (#450).** While one person runs a workspace, Treasury asks an owner or admin to invite an
  approver and the accountant, and the weekly summary adds an invite link.
- **Ask every payee for an address at once (#442).** On **Counterparties**, one step sends every vendor and contractor
  with no address of its own a payee link, emailed where the payee has a notice email.
- **Counterparties see who sent a page or email (#449).** The supplier status page, payee link, receipt and the emails
  a counterparty gets now say which business sent them, with a link back to Vestiarion tagged so the founder dashboard
  counts the workspaces it brings.
- **A new landing page (#443 to #447, #451).** Questions an owner asks first, proof that leads with one real payment on
  Arc mainnet, who holds each safety rule, what each integration does, real code for each developer way in, and a page
  that stays still at rest.
- **Smaller fixes (#448, #455).**
  - Docs tables keep names and codes whole.
  - Get started counts the USDC in the owner's own wallet as soon as the workspace goes live.
  - The README shows the two Arc mainnet to Base proofs.

## Database

One new migration, applied in production before this release: `0132_spending_limit_v2.sql`. Two existing
migrations were edited so the chain check allows `BASE` for a vendor, `0044_cctp_payouts.sql` and
`0078_mainnet_go_live.sql`. `db:migrate` re-runs every file, and the change is applied in production.
`0044_cctp_payouts.sql` now rewrites unknown chains only on its first run.

The total is 125 (`0001_init.sql` to `0132_spending_limit_v2.sql`; numbers 0091 to 0093, 0096 to 0098 and 0119 are
unused).

## Known limitations

- **Beta.** Screens, wording and features change often. The spending-limit contracts (versions 1 and 2) and the escrow
  contract have not been audited.
- **Base only, USDC only.** Arc mainnet pays across chains to Base alone; other chains, and EURC across chains, are not
  offered there yet.
- **Proofs are the team's.** Both Arc mainnet to Base payouts were made from the team's own test workspaces. No
  customer has paid a real bill on Arc mainnet yet.
- **The live slice** has not yet carried a shadow-mode business to its first real payment.
- **Bank payouts** run on BlindPay's development instance. Only vendors in Mexico, over SPEI.
- **QuickBooks Online** still connects to sandbox companies only, until Intuit's app assessment is complete.
