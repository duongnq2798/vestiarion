# Vestiarion v0.1.0-beta.8 — The agent negotiates early payment, bank payouts and Zoho

The eighth beta of Vestiarion. The agent now negotiates early payment with suppliers: on a bill it scheduled for later,
it offers to pay today for a discount it picks within the owner's ceiling. The supplier accepts, declines or proposes
a smaller discount by a one-time link. The agent answers the counter itself, within limits code sets, and pays the same
day. A vendor's bills can be paid to its bank account in Mexico, in pesos over SPEI, through BlindPay. Bills and won
deals come in from Zoho Books and Zoho CRM. And code now keeps the agent from paying a bill early when nothing is gained.

This release covers 34 merged pull requests (#375 to #408) since
[v0.1.0-beta.7](https://github.com/duongnq2798/vestiarion/releases/tag/v0.1.0-beta.7). Unlike earlier betas, not
every change in this batch was reviewed by an independent reviewer before merge. Each passed CI, and each feature was
tested end to end on the live product, where the live tests found the defects fixed in #380 to #388, #393, #394, #396,
#402 and #405. Production is deployed from `main` on every merge, so it may already run newer changes than this
release lists.

## Highlights

- **Early-payment offers (#390, #394, #396, #402).**
  - Bills & receivables lists the bills the agent will pay later under **Pay early for a discount**, each with the
    discount worth asking: twice what the cash would earn in the reserve by the due date.
  - An owner or admin offers it to the supplier through a one-time link, kept only as a hash. The supplier's page
    needs no account.
  - An accepted offer puts the discount on the bill, ending today, and the agent pays it at once, after every check.
  - Proven on Arc testnet: a 1.00% offer on a 2.00 USDC bill was paid 1.98 USDC 24 seconds after the supplier
    accepted it.
- **The agent makes the offers itself (#398).**
  - An owner or admin turns it on with the most the agent may ask.
  - Each cycle, the agent weighs each scheduled bill code allows: in USDC, a week or more out, never offered, the
    supplier reachable, and the cash there to pay it today. The model picks whether to offer and the discount, and the
    link goes to the supplier in a fixed template.
  - Proven: the agent offered 0.50% on a 0.50 USDC bill by email, and paid 0.4975 USDC 23 seconds after the supplier
    accepted.
- **Suppliers counter, and the agent answers (#404).**
  - On the agent's offer, the supplier may propose a smaller discount.
  - Code declines a counter no better than the reserve's yield, or one the cash cannot cover, without asking a model.
    The model decides the rest, the supplier gets the answer by email, and an accepted counter is paid the same day.
  - Proven: the supplier proposed 0.25% against 0.50%, and the agent accepted and paid 0.01995 USDC.
- **Results, cover, activity and a sandbox (#406, #407, #408).**
  - Bills says what the offers came to and what paying early saved, as a yearly rate.
  - The agent withdraws its own offers when the cash no longer covers them.
  - Each step shows in the activity toasts, Telegram and Slack.
  - In a sandbox, the agent's offers go to the member who turned them on, who plays the supplier. The sample data
    adds a bill for it to offer.
- **No early payment for nothing (#405).** When the model chose to pay a bill early with no discount and no shortfall,
  code now schedules it for its due date and says so in the reasoning. A discount, a due or overdue bill, or a day a
  person set still pays today.
- **Bank payouts to Mexico through BlindPay (#379 to #388).**
  - An owner sets the workspace up from Settings, accepting BlindPay's terms on BlindPay's page.
  - An owner or admin gives a vendor its CLABE, checked before BlindPay sees it.
  - The agent decides the vendor's bills as any other, and a person approves every bank payout after seeing BlindPay's
    quote.
  - On Arc testnet, BlindPay takes its test token USDB, minted from the Bank payouts card, and the agent times those
    bills against the wallet's USDB.
  - Proven on BlindPay's development instance: 10.00 USDB became 173.00 MXN over SPEI.
- **Zoho Books and Zoho CRM (#376, #377, #378).**
  - Open bills come in from a Zoho Books organization for a person to add.
  - Won deals come in from Zoho CRM with their accounts and contacts, open ones are listed as not won yet, and lost
    ones never come in.
  - Both were proven on a live organization.
- **The docs and the landing page.**
  - The docs have a new navigation, reading surface and home (#395, #397, #399).
  - The landing page groups its integrations by what each tool does, gives Arc and Circle their own pair, and leads
    each network's proof card with whoever has paid on it (#383, #384, #389, #392, #400, #403).
  - The README's Add to Chrome is drawn like its other buttons (#391).
- **Fixes.**
  - A migration re-run never narrows the payout route check again (#393).
  - The in-process test database runs on UTC (#401).
- **Release notes (#375)** for v0.1.0-beta.7.

## Database

Six new migrations, each applied in production before this release: `0122_zoho_books_connections.sql`,
`0123_zoho_crm_connections.sql`, `0124_bank_payouts.sql`, `0125_early_payment_offers.sql`,
`0126_agent_early_payment_offers.sql` and `0127_offer_counters.sql`.

`0047_milestone_escrow.sql` and `0124` now widen the payout route check only while it lacks their value. The total
is 120 (`0001_init.sql` to `0127_offer_counters.sql`; numbers 0091 to 0093, 0096 to 0098 and 0119 are unused).

## Known limitations

- **Beta.** Screens, wording and features change often. The spending-limit and escrow contracts have not been audited.
- **Bank payouts** run on BlindPay's development instance, with its test business values. Only vendors in Mexico, over
  SPEI.
- **Early-payment offers** are in USDC only. The agent offers a bill once, and asks no more than the ceiling an owner
  or admin sets.
- **QuickBooks Online** still connects to sandbox companies only, until Intuit's app assessment is complete.
- **Pipedrive and Asana** listings are prepared, not yet submitted.
