# Vestiarion v0.1.0-beta.14 — Free tools, the monthly close pack and the Quality Lab

The fourteenth beta of Vestiarion. Four free tools now run in anyone's browser, with no sign-up: a payment terms
calculator, an OFAC check for a whole vendor list, a check of a bill list for duplicates and deadlines, and the
address checker that was already there. A workspace opened from one of them is counted for it in Vestiarion's own
growth figures. For the books, an owner or their accountant gets one page per month that lists every payment Circle
confirmed, what was checked before it, and the signed ledger entry that records it. The page can be downloaded, read
through the API or sent to Google Sheets. The Quality Lab runs fixed scenarios, and the team's own shadow-mode
decisions, through the real decision code on every change, and fails CI when any of them makes a wrong payment.
Also in this release:
- a vendor's document is checked against its bill, and a note the AI reader (the workspace's model) wrote about it is
  quoted on the bill's card, marked as its words, not Vestiarion's;
- in shadow mode, the report shows how often you agreed with the agent, by decision and by vendor;
- the landing page follows the payee lock holding a payment on Arc testnet, in the team's own workspace;
- Slack lists the bills waiting for approval, each with a **Review** button;
- a PDF built to expand when opened is refused, and one that cannot be measured is read within limits;
- the repository has a contributing guide, a code of conduct and an accessibility statement.

This release covers 22 merged pull requests (#516 to #537) since
[v0.1.0-beta.13](https://github.com/duongnq2798/vestiarion/releases/tag/v0.1.0-beta.13). Each change passed CI.

## Highlights

- **Four free tools, no sign-up (#533, #534, #535, #536, #537).** Each tool runs in the browser, and nothing typed or
  pasted is sent anywhere or kept. Each page says it may count the visit and a click on **Try it on your bills**, by
  the page's address, and its start links can set a first-touch cookie (below).
  - **Payment terms calculator (#534)**, at [/payment-terms](https://www.vestiarion.xyz/payment-terms). Enter an
    invoice's date, amount and terms, such as 2/10 net 30, net 45 or net 30 EOM. It gives:
    - the due date and the discount deadline;
    - what skipping the discount costs a year, each with its formula: for 2/10 net 30, 37.2% simple on 365 days, 36.7%
      simple on 360 days and 44.6% compounded;
    - the break-even rate, and a late fee after any number of days;
    - one decision, "Pay on" a day, with its reason, from the same written policy the agent's decisions are checked
      against.

    Terms it can read only in part are refused, never guessed. Seven pages explain common terms with a worked example.
  - **OFAC vendor check (#535)**, at [/check-vendor](https://www.vestiarion.xyz/check-vendor). Type one vendor, or
    paste or drop a whole list of up to 5,000 rows.
    - Each name written in Latin letters is compared with every name and other name on OFAC's SDN list as of Oct 28,
      2026. A name written only in another script is marked not compared, not cleared.
    - Each wallet is compared with the addresses OFAC's list gives, and each 0x wallet also with the other 0x wallets
      in your list, so one made to look like another vendor's is caught.
    - A possible match says why it matched and links to Treasury's own search, and the results download as a CSV.

    It covers the SDN list only, and says it is a first look, not a compliance decision.
  - **Bill list check (#536)**, at [/check-bills](https://www.vestiarion.xyz/check-bills). Paste rows, or drop a CSV
    from QuickBooks, Xero, Zoho Books or a sheet. It lists:
    - bills that may repeat each other, scored by the agent's own duplicate rule, plus invoice numbers written
      differently (BP-1042 and bp 1042);
    - bills of the same amount about a month apart, to check each is a new month's bill;
    - early-payment discounts ending, and late fees starting, in the next 7 days, with what each is worth.

    Its sample list of made-up vendors finds 2 likely duplicates, 1,700 USD at stake.
  - **Counting which tool brought a workspace (#533).** On a tool's page, **Try it on your bills** and the footer's
    **Get started** set Vestiarion's first-party `vx_ft` cookie when the browser holds no first touch yet.
    - It holds the tool's name and page, the site that linked to it when the page was opened straight from that link,
      and the time, never anything typed.
    - A workspace opened next in that browser within 90 days is counted for that tool in Vestiarion's own growth
      figures, sign-in included. An earlier campaign's first touch is never replaced.
    - The privacy page says so. The footer has a **Free tools** group, and the sitemap lists every tool.
  - **Search engines can find them (#537).** `/robots.txt` now names the sitemap. Before, it answered with the
    not-found page.
- **The monthly close pack (#517, #519, #520, #525).**
  - **One page per month (#517).** Every payment Circle confirmed in a month, one row each, with:
    - the bill, with its invoice number and document;
    - who decided and approved it, an accountant's co-sign included;
    - what was checked before it was paid;
    - the transaction, with its explorer link;
    - the signed ledger entry that records it, verified against the workspace's keys.

    **Print or save as PDF**, **Download CSV** and **Download signed JSON**, which carries the cited entries and the
    workspace's public keys. Each download is recorded in the ledger. Open it from the **Audit log** or the accountant
    console.
  - **Google Sheets (#519).** An owner or admin connects one Google account per workspace. **Export to Google Sheets**
    writes the month to a spreadsheet in that account's Drive: a **Payments** tab with 51 columns and a **Summary**
    tab. Every cell is written as a value, so nothing runs as a formula. Vestiarion asks Google only for access to the
    files it makes.
  - **Through the API, and SDK 0.4.0 (#520).** A workspace API key reads the same signed files the pages download, with
    members named by user id instead of email:
    - one payment's evidence;
    - a paid bill's receipt;
    - the payee lock's evidence for a period;
    - a month's close pack.

    The TypeScript SDK 0.4.0, on npm, has a method for each, and `verifyEvidence` checks any of these files offline.
  - **Faster (#525).** The page starts each read as soon as what it needs is in, so its reads take three rounds
    instead of five or six. Its files are the same, byte for byte.
- **Vendor documents checked against the bill (#518, #522).**
  - While vendor requests are in shadow or on, each PDF a vendor sends for a bill's missing purchase order or goods
    receipt is read within a minute. A rule-based reader reads it, and so does the workspace's AI model when one is
    set up; that model's provider then receives the PDF's text, as the privacy page lists.
  - Code then checks the document's vendor, currency, amount, purchase order and any address it asks to be paid to
    against the bill.
  - With requests on, a match adds the purchase order and nothing else. The agent then holds the bill for a person: it
    never pays such a bill on its own.
  - A mismatch keeps the bill waiting and gives the reason, such as "The document says 8.50 USDC; the bill says 8.00
    USDC." Owners and admins can also **Attach a document** a vendor emailed them.
  - When the AI reader notes something for a person, such as a changed bank account, the card now quotes that note as
    the reader's words, cleaned of links, hidden characters and codes (#522).
- **The Quality Lab (#526, #527).**
  - **Fixed scenarios (#526).** 39 scenarios run in CI through the agent's real decision code, with the model's
    answers scripted, often to what an attacker would want: prompt injection in an invoice, a payee whose address just
    changed, OFAC's list, the limits, a repeat of a paid bill, a send whose answer was lost.
  - **A wrong payment in the lab fails CI.** Any of these counts as one:
    - a transfer or a schedule where money should stop;
    - a transfer to another address, or for another amount;
    - a second transfer for one bill.
  - **Replayed decisions (#527).** The team's own shadow-mode decisions are rebuilt from their signed ledger entries and
    replayed through today's code. Of 46 decisions: 37 reach the same outcome and 9 cannot be rebuilt from their
    record, with 0 wrong payments.
  - The results are on [Open numbers](https://www.vestiarion.xyz/open) and How the agent decides. Every scenario is in
    the docs. The Quality Lab tests the code, not the model's own judgement.
- **How often you agreed, before a live slice (#523).** In shadow mode, the report's **Before a live slice** shows how
  often you agreed with the agent, by what it decided and by vendor, each with the number of verdicts behind it. Under
  five verdicts it says **Too few to tell yet**. Each vendor shows the same figure beside its checkbox under **Start
  the live slice**. It only measures, and changes nothing the agent may do.
- **The payee lock on the landing page (#524).** The safety section names the payee lock, then follows a payment in
  the team's own workspace on Arc testnet:
  - a vendor's address changed;
  - code held the agent's 3.00 USDC payment until a second member confirmed the address, and then for the 4-hour wait;
  - only then did the agent pay.

  Each step shows when the signed ledger recorded it, and the transaction links to the explorer.
- **Bills waiting for approval in Slack (#528).**
  - While deciding payments from Slack is off (the default), the channel's message after each cycle ends with up to
    ten bills still waiting for a person, then how many more wait.
  - Each shows the amount, the payee, why it stopped, and a **Review** button that opens the bill's card on Approvals.
  - With deciding from Slack on, each stopped bill keeps its buttons as before.
  - Opening a link never approves anything. Hidden characters are removed from names and reasons, and Slack is told to
    make no link or mention from text inside them.
- **Smaller changes (#531, #532).** The repository has a contributing guide, a code of conduct (the Contributor
  Covenant 2.1), an accessibility statement that names WCAG 2.2 AA as a target, not a claim, issue forms and a pull
  request template (#531). The README's banner is redrawn from the landing page's hero, with a matching image for
  GitHub's social preview (#532).

## What changed

What a workspace notices without turning anything on:
- A PDF whose contents would expand to more than 16 MB is refused before it is read: "This PDF could not be read.
  Paste the invoice's text instead." A PDF that cannot be measured, such as an encrypted one, is read on its own within
  15 seconds, 50 pages and a memory limit. Only the first three PDFs attached to an `.eml` file are read (#529).
- Disconnecting an integration shows its confirmation as a toast. Before, the confirmation disappeared with the
  button (#530).
- The Slack channel's message lists the bills still waiting, and no longer repeats them among the decisions (#528).
- The footer's links to the landing page's sections work from every page (#534).
- The close pack page opens faster (#525).

For integrators and self-hosted deployments:
- `/api/v1` adds five reads: `GET /api/v1/invoices/{id}/evidence`, `GET /api/v1/milestones/{id}/evidence`,
  `GET /api/v1/invoices/{id}/receipt`, `GET /api/v1/payee-lock/evidence` and `GET /api/v1/close-packs/{month}`.
  - A key makes up to 10 of these reads at once, then one every 6 seconds.
  - Reading records nothing in the ledger.
  - The MCP server does not offer them yet.
- The ledger and webhooks:
  - three new ledger entries: `document_checked`, `close_pack_exported` and `close_pack_export_failed`;
  - `document_received` can now be a member's;
  - the agent's pay and schedule decisions on a bill whose purchase order came from a vendor's document are held under
    a new rule, `invoice.vendor_document`.

  The [API changelog](https://www.vestiarion.xyz/docs/changelog) lists each change in five entries dated Oct 25 and
  Oct 26.
- The TypeScript SDK 0.4.0 is on npm. The CLI stays at 0.1.0.
- Google Sheets needs `GOOGLE_SHEETS_CLIENT_ID` and `GOOGLE_SHEETS_CLIENT_SECRET`. Without them, Settings shows no
  Google Sheets card and `/api/integrations/googlesheets/*` answers 404. With them, until migration 0145 runs, the
  card says Google Sheets is not set up yet.
- Apply the migrations below before upgrading.

## Known limitations

- **Beta.** Screens, wording and features change often. The spending-limit contracts and the escrow contract have not
  been audited. Their source is verified, which is not an audit.
- **Not every change was reviewed by an independent reviewer.** 3 of the 22 pull requests, listed above, record no
  independent review before merge.
- **No customer has paid a real bill on Arc mainnet yet.**
- **The free tools are a first look.**
  - The OFAC check covers the SDN list only, as of its snapshot's date. It cannot see who owns a company, and it does
    not compare a name written in a script other than Latin letters.
  - The bill list check does not know which bills were paid.
  - The calculator does not know the local rules on late fees.
- **The bill list check waits for the browser to draw a frame before it checks.** A tab the browser does not draw,
  such as one hidden in the background, does not run it until it is shown.
- **Search engines have not indexed the free tools yet.** The sitemap was submitted on Oct 28.
- **The Quality Lab tests the decision code, not the model's judgement.**
  - It covers USDC bills on Arc only: not milestones, EURC, payouts to other chains or bank payouts.
  - 9 of the 46 replayed decisions cannot be rebuilt from their record, and are run both ways instead.
- **PDFs that can be measured are still read without a time limit.** This applies to PDFs read from a document, an
  email, the Chrome extension, Telegram, Slack or the payee link. Such a PDF is read on the request itself, so a small
  file built to be slow to read can hold that request for about a minute and a half (#529).
- **Vendor documents:** an image, a scan, a PDF over 4 MB or of more than 50 pages, or a password-protected one is
  not checked. A goods receipt is never marked from a document.
- **Checks the pull requests left undone:**
  - posting the waiting list, with its **Review** links, to a real Slack workspace (#528);
  - following a workspace credited to a free tool through sign-in and workspace creation in a real browser, where the
    cookie write is tested only in a simulated browser (#533);
  - whether the project's X account accepts direct messages from accounts it does not follow. The code of conduct and
    the accessibility statement send reports there (#531).
- **The MCP server** does not offer the evidence, receipt and close pack reads yet.
- **QuickBooks Online** still connects to sandbox companies only, until Intuit's app assessment is complete.
- **Bank payouts** run on BlindPay's development instance, to vendors in Mexico over SPEI only.
