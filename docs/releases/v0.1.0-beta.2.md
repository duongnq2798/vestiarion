# Vestiarion v0.1.0-beta.2 — Collections, projects and accountants

The second beta of Vestiarion, an AI treasury agent that pays a small business's bills in USDC on Arc inside limits it
cannot cross, with every decision signed into a ledger. This release adds the receivables side a small business runs
every week: customer statements, collections that follow the customer, and receipts written back to HubSpot. It also
adds deal and project budgets, and a role and a console for the outside accountant.

It names what [www.vestiarion.xyz](https://www.vestiarion.xyz) runs as of the commit below, which is 13 merged pull
requests (#326 to #338) since [v0.1.0-beta.1](https://github.com/duongnq2798/vestiarion/releases/tag/v0.1.0-beta.1).
Each feature was reviewed by an independent reviewer before merge, and the defects found were fixed with a test that
failed first. Production is deployed from `main` on every merge, so it may already run newer changes than this release
lists.

## Highlights

- **Customer statements (#333).** An owner or admin sends a customer a link to its own statement. It needs no account
  and no wallet, and works for 7, 30 or 90 days. On it the customer can:
  - see what it owes per currency, with part payments included;
  - pay an invoice on Arc through its pay link;
  - say it paid, promise a day, or dispute an invoice, in a thread with the business.

  A claim of payment is only the customer's word and never marks anything paid. Links are stored hashed and can be
  stopped at any time.
- **Collections that follow the customer (#338).** Before reminding a customer, the agent weighs what Vestiarion knows
  about it: disputes, claims, promises, part payments, the reminders already sent, and the HubSpot deal it was sold
  through. Each receivable gets one next step with the facts behind it.
  - Each customer can have its own rules: ask first, the strongest tone, and the days between reminders.
  - A broken promise, or a claim still unconfirmed after a week, goes to finance instead of an automatic reminder.
  - Every email is sent once, even when it is retried.
- **Receipts back in HubSpot (#332).** Once an owner allows it, each deal planned from HubSpot gets Vestiarion's figures
  written back: collected, still owed, the last receipt with its Arc transaction, and the reconciliation status.
  - Only money settled on Arc counts.
  - Every write is read back from HubSpot.
  - A value someone changed in HubSpot is shown as a conflict and never overwritten.
- **Deal cash readiness and held-bill owners (#331).** A deal takes its planned costs and shows day by day the first day
  it runs short and by how much, plus its estimated margin. Held bills show their priority, the day they must be decided
  by, and who can decide them.
- **Projects with their own budget (#337).** The agent sets each project bill's money aside before paying or scheduling
  it; a bill the budget cannot cover waits for a person. Two decisions at the same moment never set aside the same
  money. A bill and a milestone for the same work are not paid twice.
- **An accountant role and console (#336).** The new `accountant` role:
  - reads everything;
  - books Arc payments in Xero;
  - applies money received;
  - answers customers.

  It decides no payment and changes no treasury, limit, integration or member. `/accountant` gives one queue across
  every workspace the person belongs to, with a monthly hand-off export per workspace. Settings shows **Usage this
  month**: model calls, Circle transfers, x402 purchases, emails and connector syncs.

## Database

Six new migrations, `0107_crm_receipts.sql` to `0112_accountants.sql`, bring the total to 106 (`0001_init.sql` to
`0112_accountants.sql`; numbers 0091 to 0093 and 0096 to 0098 are unused).

| Migration | Adds |
| --- | --- |
| `0107` | The HubSpot write-back outbox |
| `0108` | Deal costs and approval escalations |
| `0109` | Customer statement links and requests |
| `0110` | Collection rules, tasks and email deliveries |
| `0111` | Projects and budget reservations |
| `0112` | The accountant role and usage records |

All are additive and idempotent, and are applied in filename order by `npm run db:migrate`. `0112` redefines
`role_rank` and `can_assign_role` from `0021`, so apply every file in order. Publishing a release never runs migrations.

## Known limitations

- **Beta.** Screens, wording and features change often. The spending-limit and escrow contracts have not been audited.
- **Arc mainnet is USDC on Arc only**, as in v0.1.0-beta.1. EURC, the USYC reserve, escrow, CCTP and Gateway payouts,
  x402 and shadow mode are Arc testnet only.
- **HubSpot write-back** sends Arc testnet and shadow-mode figures only to a HubSpot developer test account or sandbox.
  An account that approved Vestiarion before needs its super admin to allow the two optional write permissions first.
- **Accountants and Xero.** Accountants book in Xero only on a connection made after this release; older connections
  keep bookings with owners and admins until they are reconnected.
- **Usage costs.** A model's cost shows as not known unless its price is configured; usage is a record, not a bill.
- **Project budgets** are an earmark in Vestiarion, not money locked on chain. A person's Approve and pay can take a
  project over its budget.
- **QuickBooks Online** still connects to sandbox companies only, until Intuit's app assessment is complete.
