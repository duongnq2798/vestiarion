# Vestiarion v0.1.0-beta.16 — Jira, counterparty pages, a track record, Zapier and a status page

The sixteenth beta of Vestiarion. Contractor work delivered in Jira is accepted by a person before its milestone is
paid, as it already is from Asana and Linear. Every contractor, vendor and client has a page of its own, with what waits
on you and what waits on them. The agent's track record, every verdict you gave on a real bill with the signed entries
behind it, can be confirmed by your accountant with one signed button. A private Zapier integration reads the agent's
decisions and adds bills, and the n8n nodes are verified and on n8n Cloud. A public status page shows what the
production check finds. Every button and page now shows it is working in the same way, and a quick action shows
nothing. A sixth free tool checks an invoice's payment details, and a free quiz asks whether you would pay ten bills.
Also in this release:
- Next.js 16.4.6 and the MCP server SDK 2.4.0, which fix published advisories;
- Dependabot opens the week's dependency updates;
- four more short videos in the docs.

## Highlights

- **Contractor work accepted from Jira (#576, #591).** An owner or admin connects one Jira Cloud site in Settings, after
  agreeing to what Vestiarion reads there. On Contractors, a milestone is linked to the Jira issue its work is
  delivered in, by the issue's link or its key (such as `ENG-123`), with acceptance criteria written in Vestiarion.
  - When the issue is done in Jira, and not resolved as Won't Do, Duplicate or the like, a person checks the
    deliverables against the criteria and accepts the work. Only then does the agent decide the payment, under every
    guardrail. Anyone on a Jira site can move an issue to Done, so a done issue alone never pays.
  - An acceptance by the payee is refused, matched by email, and by Atlassian account where Jira finds one for the
    payee's email.
  - Reopening the issue, resolving it as not done or deleting it, or changing its deliverables, takes the acceptance
    back when Vestiarion reads the change before the payment is sent.
  - Vestiarion writes nothing to Jira but one webhook on the site, and keeps no Atlassian account ID, name or email.
    One work tool verifies a milestone: Asana, Linear and Jira each refuse a milestone linked in another.
  - Proven in the team's own test workspace on Arc testnet on Nov 11, with a free Jira Cloud site: a 0.50 test USDC
    milestone was linked to an issue, the issue was moved to Done and read through the webhook about 20 seconds later,
    a person accepted the work, and the agent paid it (#591). Reopening the issue after payment changed nothing on the
    card, as intended. That is our own testing, not a customer's.
- **One page for each counterparty (#574, #575, #594, #597).** Every member of a workspace can open a page for each
  contractor, vendor or client, from **Open their page** on its row on Counterparties.
  - From the top: what is owed to them and what was paid to them, each per currency, never USDC and EURC added
    together; their profile; their payment address in full, never cut short; for an owner or admin, their payee link
    with the latest five links; their milestones; their bills, or a client's invoices; their payments, each paid bill
    with where its receipt is; the documents asked of them; and the latest changes to them, each linked to its signed
    entry in the Audit log.
  - **Needs you** and **Waiting on them** (#594), under the tiles, say what waits on your side, such as a changed
    address to confirm, and what waits on them, such as documents asked for. Each line links to the part that holds it.
  - A client's page lists **Refunds to them** and the latest 10 **Refunded** (#597). The agent never pays a client, so
    each refund waits for a person in Approvals.
  - Nothing on the page pays or records anything new: its controls are the ones Counterparties, Contractors and Bills &
    receivables already have. Another workspace's counterparty is not found, like one that does not exist.
  - Every "fix it" link, such as **Edit limit**, **Confirm address** or **Review screening** under a stopped bill, now
    opens its part of the page (#574). A milestone named in Slack, Telegram, the console's notices or a stuck-payment
    email opens its own row on Contractors (#575).
- **The agent's track record, for your accountant to confirm (#573).** A page linked from the report and the
  accountant console shows every verdict you gave on a real bill, with the decision it judged and the signed entries
  both rest on, how often you agreed by decision and by vendor, and the vendors that earned your rule. It downloads as a
  JSON file that carries each entry in full. Your accountant confirms it with one button, which signs their
  confirmation in the ledger against the record's SHA-256; a confirmation names the record it confirmed until a new
  verdict changes it. It only measures: no payment, rule, limit or setting reads a confirmation.
- **Zapier, and n8n on n8n Cloud (#563, #564, #566).**
  - **Zapier (#563, #564).** A private integration, joined by invitation. Its triggers are New Agent Decision, with the
    agent's reasoning; Bill Paid, by the agent or once a person approves; and New Ledger Entry, filtered by domain,
    actor or action. Its action Create Bill adds a payable for the agent to decide, with an idempotency key so a
    replayed Zap adds it once. It connects with a workspace API key, and never approves or pays. Version 1.1.0 was
    pushed to Zapier and tested live on the team's test workspace: the connection, New Agent Decision, Bill Paid and
    Create Bill (#564). That is our own testing, not a customer's.
  - **n8n (#566).** n8n verified `@vestiarion/n8n-nodes-vestiarion`, and it is live on n8n Cloud: search for Vestiarion
    in the canvas, with **Verified community nodes** on in the Admin Panel, and nothing to install. The
    [n8n guide](https://www.vestiarion.xyz/docs/get-started/n8n#install) says so.
- **A public status page (#560, #561)**, at [/status](https://www.vestiarion.xyz/status). It shows whether Vestiarion's
  public pages are up, as the production check finds them from GitHub's servers, outside Vercel and Supabase, about
  every 10 minutes.
  - For each page: its last result and time, how many times it was up of the times checked in the last 24 hours and 7
    days, with the checks missed, and an hour-by-hour strip where an hour with no check shows as not checked, never as
    up.
  - When the last check is more than 25 minutes old, the headline says monitoring paused, and each page shows its last
    result as it was then.
  - It lists the incidents of the last 7 days and says what it does not check.
  - The check publishes the last 7 days of rounds to the repository's `status` branch, whose README shows the same state
    on GitHub while the site does not answer. The page reads that file from GitHub, nothing from Vestiarion's database.
- **Waiting looks the same everywhere, and never blinks (#599, #600).**
  - A button is taken the moment it is pressed and cannot be pressed twice. Its spinner appears only once the work has
    run for 150 ms and then stays for at least half a second, so an action that answers at once shows no change.
  - Following a link draws a thin bar along the top once the next page takes longer than a moment.
  - While **Approve and pay** is sending, **Return to agent**, **Reject** and **Add details** wait, and while a
    milestone pays, **Close** waits, so no second decision races a payment.
  - Connecting an integration takes one press.
  - A form whose action never answers keeps what was typed and says it may not have gone through, instead of an error
    screen. A page outside a workspace that fails to load shows the site's own error screen, with Try again and a link
    to the status page.
  - The agent's line in every workspace header gives up on a request that hangs, and says "not updating, retrying"
    after three misses in a row.
- **A free invoice details check, and a free quiz (#562, #568).**
  - **The invoice details check**, a sixth free tool, at [/check-invoice](https://www.vestiarion.xyz/check-invoice).
    Put a vendor's payment details on file beside the ones on their new invoice, and paste the message that came with
    it. A changed wallet, IBAN, account or routing code is a stop, as is a sender's domain made to look like the
    vendor's. IBAN check digits, a US routing number's check digit and a SWIFT / BIC's form are checked. The message is
    read for a request to change payment details, pressure, or a request not to call. Then the page writes the call to
    make, to the number on file and never one from the invoice. It runs in the browser: nothing is sent or kept.
  - **Would you pay this bill?**, at [/would-you-pay](https://www.vestiarion.xyz/would-you-pay): ten bills from the
    Quality Lab, written as a person would see them. Answer Pay it or Stop it, then see what Vestiarion did and why.
    The right answers come from the lab's scenarios and what Vestiarion did from its last run, so the quiz cannot drift
    from what CI proves. Nothing is sent or kept.

## What changed

What a workspace notices without turning anything on:
- Analytics no longer sees which record a page is about: every id in a workspace page's address reaches Google
  Analytics as `:id` (#574). The [privacy page](https://www.vestiarion.xyz/privacy#analytics) says so, and also that
  while Google Analytics' enhanced measurement is on, which is Google's default, a click on a link to a block explorer
  sends it that link's address, which can hold a payee's wallet address.
- Four more short videos in the docs, each at the top of its page with its transcript, recorded on Nov 7 (#570, #571):
  the Quality Lab and the quiz, filmed on their public pages; the monthly close pack for your accountant; a vendor's
  missing document, checked against the bill; and the autonomy ladder. Each is also on YouTube, linked from the README.
  Nothing was approved, paid or changed while filming.
- Asking a vendor for a missing document has a guide of its own (#570), and the report guide has screenshots of How
  often you agreed and Earned vendors (#572).
- The Asana, Linear and Jira guides say that a change in the work tool after payment does not show on the card (#591).
- Keyboard focus in the docs (#569): the copy button on a code block shows a white focus ring on the dark panel, and a
  table wider than the screen can be reached and scrolled with the keyboard.
- The production check and the status page cover the invoice details check and the quiz too: 12 pages (#562, #568).
- Next.js 16.4.6, up from 16.3.6, which fixes the advisories published against 16.3.6, among them a remote code
  execution in image optimization when SVG files are served, a denial of service through Server Actions and a
  server-side request forgery in image optimization (#577). Vestiarion serves no SVG through image optimization, but
  it does use Server Actions and `next/image`.
- The remote MCP server runs on `@modelcontextprotocol/server` 2.4.0, up from 2.2.0, with `mcp-handler` 2.3.1 (#578).
  2.2.0 had two high advisories; Vestiarion's MCP server builds a fresh server for each request rather than sharing
  one. x402 moves to 2.30 and zod to 4.6.7, for zod's memory-exhaustion advisory. The tools and their answers are
  unchanged, and nothing changes in how the app looks or works.
- The Security page says Dependabot opens weekly pull requests that never merge on their own (#578).

For integrators and self-hosted deployments:
- `GET /api/v1/ledger` takes `order=desc`, the newest entries first, with no cursor (#563). The TypeScript SDK 0.4.2,
  on npm, accepts it (#567). The Postman collection's **List the agent's decisions** reads newest first; the SDK's
  `ledger.list` pages, the CLI and the MCP server keep reading oldest first.
- `GET /api/v1/milestones` can return `jira` as a milestone's `verificationMethod` (#576). SDK 0.4.3 types the value; it
  is packed in the repository and not yet published to npm. A milestone is not linked to a Jira issue through the API,
  and the write API still adds a milestone pending.
- The ledger:
  - for Jira, `jira_issue_linked`, `jira_issue_unlinked`, `jira_issue_ready`, `jira_work_accepted` and
    `jira_acceptance_withdrawn`, none of which carries an Atlassian account ID, name or email;
    `milestone_criteria_set` is recorded for a milestone linked to Jira, `accounting_connected` can name
    `provider: "jira"`, and disconnecting signs `accounting_disconnected` with `revokedAtProvider: false`;
  - for the track record, `track_record_exported` and `track_record_confirmed` (#573).

  The [API changelog](https://www.vestiarion.xyz/docs/changelog) lists the API changes in two entries, dated Nov 6 and
  Nov 9.
- The track record's file is `GET /api/evidence/track-record?org=` (`vestiarion-track-record/1`), outside `/api/v1`,
  ten downloads per ten minutes. Confirming it takes the new permission `track_record.confirm`, held by accountants.
- New settings: `JIRA_CLIENT_ID` and `JIRA_CLIENT_SECRET`, from an Atlassian OAuth 2.0 (3LO) app with
  resource-restricted tokens and the classic scopes `read:jira-work`, `read:jira-user` and `manage:jira-webhook`.
  Without them there is no Jira card, the connect page says Jira is not set up on this deployment yet, and the Jira
  webhook route answers 404.
- The production check's job gains `contents: write`, to publish to the `status` branch. The token goes only to the
  script's own step, which fetches the script with `curl` instead of `actions/checkout`, and the job's cache actions are
  pinned to a commit (#560). A fork must be public for the `curl` step, and name its own repository in
  `STATUS_FILE_URL` (`src/lib/status/file.ts`) and `GITHUB_URL` (`src/lib/site-links.ts`).
- The Zapier integration lives in `zapier/`, with its own CI job, which runs its tests and `zapier-platform validate`
  (#563). A version is pushed to Zapier by the owner, by hand, never from CI (#564).
- A test holds `next`, `@next/mdx` and `eslint-config-next` to one exact version, and the lock to carry the Linux build
  of the compiler that CI and Vercel use. Each docs route now traces about 1,060 files, down from about 4,650 (#577).
- Dependabot opens the week's updates each Monday, for npm and GitHub Actions: minor and patch updates as one pull
  request, the Next.js packages as one and Circle's SDKs as one (#578, #593). Circle's SDK majors, the Solidity
  compiler the contracts were verified with, and Vestiarion's own SDK are left to be upgraded by hand. The SDK, CLI,
  n8n and Zapier packages, which are published by hand, are left out. The release notes GitHub generates gain a
  Dependencies section.
- Dependency updates in this release, opened by Dependabot:
  - #579, GitHub Actions: `actions/checkout` and `actions/setup-node` 7, `actions/upload-artifact` 7,
    `actions/download-artifact` 8 and `actions/cache` 6.1.0;
  - #581, eslint 10.14.0, a major version;
  - #583, unpdf 2.1.0, which reads PDF documents, a major version;
  - #584, `@anthropic-ai/sdk` 1.6.0, which the agent's decision uses, a major version;
  - #585, happy-dom 21.0.4, used by the tests, a major version;
  - #588, follow-redirects 1.16.3, and #592, vite 8.3.8;
  - #595, 17 minor and patch updates, among them Circle's `@circle-fin/app-kit` 1.21.0 and `@circle-fin/earn-kit`
    1.13.0, React 19.3.1, viem 2.57.17 and supabase-js 2.119.0.
- Packages: SDK 0.4.2 on npm, and 0.4.3 packed but not yet published; CLI 0.2.0 and the n8n nodes 0.1.3, unchanged;
  the Zapier integration 1.1.0.

## Known limitations

- **Beta.** Screens, wording and features change often. The spending-limit contracts and the escrow contract have not
  been audited. Their source is verified, which is not an audit.
- **Not every change was reviewed by an independent reviewer.** 29 of the 34 pull requests, listed above, record no
  independent review before merge; 8 of them are Dependabot's dependency updates.
- **No customer has paid a real bill on Arc mainnet yet.**
- **Jira** is set up on vestiarion.xyz but not open to every workspace yet: the Atlassian app is not shared, and
  Atlassian lets only its own team allow it (#591). The pull requests record one live run (#591); the design's check
  with a second Atlassian account waits until the app is shared. Jira's logo is not shown, and linking and accepting
  happen in the console only, not through the API, Telegram, Slack or MCP. Atlassian gives apps no way to end their own
  access, so disconnecting deletes the webhook and the tokens, and says to remove Vestiarion from Connected apps in the
  Atlassian account.
- **Paid milestones are no longer read.** Asana, Linear and Jira stop reading a link once its milestone is paid or
  closed, so a change in the work tool after payment does not show on the card (#591).
- **The TypeScript SDK 0.4.3**, which types `jira`, is packed in the repository but not yet published to npm.
- **Zapier** is private and joined by invitation until Zapier reviews it.
- **The track record:** the accountant's confirmation is proven by tests; #573 records no confirmation made in
  production. A confirmation by someone who is not a member of the workspace, an API endpoint and a PDF are not in this
  version.
- **Waiting:** the rules were checked in a local development server for #599 and by tests for #600; neither pull
  request records a check of the signed-in screens in production.
- **Analytics:** while Google Analytics' enhanced measurement is on, a click on a link to a block explorer sends that
  link's address, which can hold a payee's wallet address. The privacy page says so.
- **Circle's SDKs** still carry advisories that `npm audit` lists, whose only offered fix is a major version, to be
  upgraded by hand (#578).
- **Earned vendors** are shadow mode and Arc testnet only, as in v0.1.0-beta.15. Two syncs at the same moment can sign
  the same status twice, and a Disagree given while a cycle runs takes effect at its next stage. The cash priority plan
  does not run in shadow mode.
- **Approve many:** the operating wallet must hold the total, since a selection never brings cash back from the
  reserve. If a send fails while a batch is claimed, its bills stay submitting for about 15 minutes. Slack still
  approves one bill at a time; Telegram, Discord and the API approve none.
- **Linear:** if Linear cannot be read when Vestiarion checks an accepted issue, the acceptance stands as the person
  gave it. Linear's logo is not shown yet, and who completed the issue is not recorded: the card's "completed by" and
  the ledger entries leave it empty.
- **Google Drive:** until Google verifies the read-only Drive permission, Google shows its "Google hasn't verified this
  app" screen, and at most 100 people can grant it. Pictures, scans, Google Docs files, shortcuts, files over 4 MB and
  subfolders are not read, and a folder with more than 1,000 files is refused.
- **The accountant console** does not list bills brought in from Xero, QuickBooks or Zoho Books yet.
- **The CLI:** rerunning an export whose default file exists spends the read before refusing.
- **The n8n nodes:** a workflow saved with 0.1.2 needs its moved fields set again in 0.1.3. There is no polling trigger
  yet, and the webhook endpoint is added by hand in Settings.
- **The production check and the status page.** The check still has no daily heartbeat on its Discord channel, and
  GitHub has started its runs hours late; the status page now says when monitoring paused. The page reads its results
  from GitHub, so when GitHub does not answer it says the results could not be read, though production may be fine.
  Its history is 7 days. It does not check the API, signed-in pages, the agent's work, payments, Circle or Arc. The
  check's `contents: write` is the repository's: held only by the script's step, it could still merge a pull request
  whose `verify` passed, and narrowing it further needs the owner's settings (#560).
- **The free tools are a first look.**
  - The OFAC checks cover the SDN list only, as of its snapshot's date. They cannot see who owns a company, and do not
    compare a name written in a script other than Latin letters.
  - The invoice details check does not know who owns an account, a wallet or a domain, and looks nothing up: no bank,
    no WHOIS, no ENS. It does not read a PDF: the details are typed or pasted. Nothing found means its checks found
    nothing, not that the invoice is the vendor's.
  - The payout batch check does not look up ENS names or check addresses that are not EVM ones.
  - The bill list check does not know which bills were paid, and the calculator does not know the local rules on late
    fees.
- **PDFs that can be measured are still read without a time limit,** as in v0.1.0-beta.15. This applies to PDFs read
  from a document, an email, a Google Drive folder, the Chrome extension, Telegram, Slack or the payee link.
- **Vendor documents:** an image, a scan, a PDF over 4 MB or of more than 50 pages, or a password-protected one is
  not checked. A goods receipt is never marked from a document.
- **Checks that v0.1.0-beta.15 listed as undone, with none recorded as done since:** posting the waiting list, with its
  **Review** links, to a real Slack workspace (#528); following a workspace credited to a free tool through sign-in in
  a real browser (#533); and whether the project's X account accepts direct messages from accounts it does not follow
  (#531).
- **The Quality Lab tests the decision code, not the model's judgement.** It covers USDC bills on Arc only, and 9 of
  the 55 replayed decisions cannot be rebuilt from their record, so they are run both ways instead. The replay does
  not test the earned-vendor gate.
- **QuickBooks Online** still connects to sandbox companies only, until Intuit's app assessment is complete.
- **Bank payouts** run on BlindPay's development instance, to vendors in Mexico over SPEI only.
