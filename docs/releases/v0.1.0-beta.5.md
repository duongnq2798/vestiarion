# Vestiarion v0.1.0-beta.5 — Commissions, payment exceptions and one answer to "is this real money?"

The fifth beta of Vestiarion. Sales partners earn commission only on money customers actually paid on Arc. A payment
that does not settle cleanly now says who acts and what to do, and paying again always looks for the earlier transfer
first. Every page now gives the same answer to whether a payment is real, and the console asks why, in one optional
line, when someone goes against the agent.

This release covers 6 merged pull requests (#351, and #353 to #357) since
[v0.1.0-beta.4](https://github.com/duongnq2798/vestiarion/releases/tag/v0.1.0-beta.4). Each feature was reviewed by an
independent reviewer before merge, and every defect found was fixed with a test that failed first. Production is
deployed from `main` on every merge, so it may already run newer changes than this release lists.

## Highlights

- **Commissions on money received (#353).** An owner or admin sets a plan for a sales partner or referrer on file: the
  rate, net of fees or gross, every deal or chosen deals, and a hold window.
  - A commission is recorded only when a customer's payment confirmed on Arc is applied to an invoice of a covered
    deal, for exactly the amount applied, with its formula shown. A partial payment earns a partial commission.
  - After the hold window, a person reviews what is due and turns it into one payable, which the agent decides and pays
    like any other bill, under every guardrail.
  - A reversed payment records a negative adjustment carried into the partner's next payout; nothing is ever pulled
    back from a wallet. The partner can never approve their own payout, enforced in the database.
  - Live on Arc testnet: a customer paid a 2 USDC invoice, the 0.20 USDC commission (2.00 USDC received × 10%) was
    recorded 9 seconds after the payment was matched, and the partner was paid and confirmed on Arc in 2 seconds.
- **What to do when a payment does not settle (#356).** Each exceptional state (slow to confirm, outcome unknown,
  failed or cancelled, not sent, a Gateway payout reported failed) says who acts, what Vestiarion already did, what to
  do next and where the trail is: on the bill's card, in Treasury's payments in flight, in Approvals and in the
  stuck-payment email, with a new guide, "When a payment does not settle".
  - Approve and pay and Pay now look for an unanswered send before anything else. A transfer Circle has is only
    recorded; one Circle has not listed yet is refused with a time to try again; only a send Circle never took is paid
    as a new payment, through every check.
  - A milestone paid in one transaction with others is never closed while that transaction may have paid it.
- **One answer to "is this real money?" (#357).** Every page that says what a payment does (the header, Settings, Agree
  and pay, Approve and pay, Pay now, reports, payee pages, receipts, the reserve, /open and `GET /api/v1/status`) now
  reads one source and uses the same words for live on Arc mainnet, live on Arc testnet, simulated, shadow mode, and
  unavailable (switched off or held). `GET /api/v1/status` answers `unavailable` while payments are switched off.
- **Asking why, in the moment (#357).** When a person rejects or returns a bill, pays one the agent held anyway, or
  disagrees in shadow mode, the console asks why in one optional line, never required. Owners and admins see the latest
  reasons on Insights; the privacy page says what is kept and who sees it.
- **Smaller changes (#354, #355).** The header names the page you are on inside a section (for example Bills &
  receivables › Commissions, or a deal's title), and the New deal form's fields line up.

## Database

Two new migrations bring the total to 113 (`0001_init.sql` to `0120_decision_reasons.sql`; numbers 0091 to 0093, 0096
to 0098 and 0119 are unused):

| Migration | Adds |
| --- | --- |
| `0118_commissions.sql` | Commission plans, their deals, commission lines and payouts, the functions that set plans, record commissions and create payouts, and the partner's refusal in the approval claim |
| `0120_decision_reasons.sql` | The reasons people give for going against the agent, and a team-only reading of them |

Both are additive and idempotent, and are applied in filename order by `npm run db:migrate`. Publishing a release never
runs migrations.

## Developer packages

`@vestiarion/sdk` 0.3.4 describes the `unavailable` status while payments are switched off; its types are unchanged.

## Known limitations

- **Beta.** Screens, wording and features change often. The spending-limit and escrow contracts have not been audited.
- **Commissions.** When the payout's creator and the partner it pays are a workspace's only approvers, a payout the
  agent holds can be approved by neither: give the partner a role that does not approve payments, or add another
  approver. Refunds paid as payables are not linked back to a commission; reverse the application instead.
- **CCTP and Gateway payouts** whose send went unanswered cannot be looked up by reference; Approve and pay asks Circle
  again under the same payment key, which Circle takes only once.
- **QuickBooks Online** still connects to sandbox companies only, until Intuit's app assessment is complete.
